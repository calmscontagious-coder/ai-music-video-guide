Frequently asked questions

Which program should a beginner start with?+

The two-day dumbbell-only program or the beginner leg program. Both run eight weeks at two sessions a week, which matches the frequency that public-health guidelines set as the floor for muscle-strengthening work. Pick the dumbbell program if you train at home and the leg program if you have gym access and want legs first.

Is blood flow restriction training safe for everyone?+

No. BFR uses cuffs to reduce blood flow during light lifting, and the research literature lists people who should not use it without medical clearance, including anyone with a history of blood clots, uncontrolled high blood pressure or pregnancy. Our BFR guide quotes the published contraindication list. Ask a clinician before your first session.

How long are the sessions?+

Only the 15-minute program states a session length on its page. The other programs list exercises and sets, not minutes; a two-session-a-week program typically fits a lunch hour, and the five-day total-body programs are full gym sessions. Rest times are taught inside each program.

Do I need a full gym?+

Not for the dumbbell-only program, which needs dumbbells, a bench and floor space. The BFR, leg, core and total-body programs name cables, machines, a pull-up bar and a barbell in their exercise lists, so they assume a gym or a well-equipped home setup. Each product page lists the equipment per exercise.
