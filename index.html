With Purpose with Presence What agencies often give you

  1. Approvals

    With us: One batched message a week. Reply yes, or change a line.

    Often elsewhere: Status calls and long threads to chase.

  2. Speed

    With us: An AI assistant drafts, builds and runs the checks; the director signs off. You see work sooner, and pay less for it.

    Often elsewhere: Weeks between a request and the change.

  3. Ownership

    With us: Every account in your business’s name, on your card, with your data. We are a user you can remove.

    Often elsewhere: Accounts set up under the agency, hard to take back when you leave.

  4. Reports

    With us: Tracking live from the start, a Monday digest, and a one-page monthly report that names the next change.

    Often elsewhere: Long reports full of charts, with no next step.

  5. Updates

    With us: Price, hours, service and photo changes in your plan, done within two business days.

    Often elsewhere: Small changes billed as extras.

  6. Seasons

    With us: Seasonal looks switched on schedule in every plan. Growth and Signature add four new looks.

    Often elsewhere: One redesign, then the same site for years.

  7. Front-desk assistant

    With us: An online front-desk assistant that answers from your own notes at any hour, hands the rest to staff and says it is not a person. In Signature; any plan can add it.

    Often elsewhere: A generic chat widget, or nothing.

  8. Price

    With us: Each plan under the market low for the same work. Growth is $1,750 a month against a $3,400 market low.

    Often elsewhere: The typical market rate: about $7,082 a month for the same Growth lines.

  9. Promises

    With us: The work in your plan, to the standards on this page, and a report on what changed. No ranking or sales guarantees.

    Often elsewhere: Guaranteed first-page rankings that nobody controls.

      08 / questions

      Questions owners ask

      Short answers on cost, the free check, ownership, AI and results. The written agreement is what counts; these match it.

      How much does it cost?

      Three monthly plans. Essentials is $950 a month plus $2,500 setup. Growth is $1,750 a month plus $5,200 setup. Signature is $2,450 a month plus $6,500 setup. Each has a 3-month minimum, then runs month to month. Your own software is billed by each vendor to your card, never through us.

      What is in the free check, and what happens after?

      A written check by email within 3 business days: your Google Business Profile, five local questions asked in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google’s AI answers, your home page on a phone, your name, address, hours and phone across five listings, and the three fixes we would make first.

      It costs nothing and does not commit you to a plan. You also get the 12-point local business website checklist straight away. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

      Who owns the accounts and the data?

      You do. Your domain, hosting, store, analytics, email platform, forms and ad accounts are in your business’s legal name, on your card. We are added as a user you can remove at any time. If you leave, you keep everything, and we hand over the theme, the files and a written list of every automation we set up.

      Do you use AI, and who checks the work?

      Yes, openly. A director, a person, runs your account, signs off every piece of work and does the photo days. We use software, AI tools included, to draft and build, and we say so openly.

      Before you see anything your customers will see, it passes our design, accessibility, speed and fact checks. Then the director signs it off, and nothing goes live until you say yes.

      Do you guarantee results?

      No. We do not promise booking or sales numbers, rankings, review counts or mentions in AI answers. We promise the work in your plan, done to the standards on this page, and a monthly report that shows what changed and what we will try next.

      Is there a contract, and how do I cancel?

      A written service agreement is signed before any work starts: scope, fees, term, what you provide and by when, ownership, confidentiality and termination. Setup is paid half on signing and half at launch; monthly fees are paid in advance on the 1st.

      After the 3-month minimum the plan runs month to month, so you can stop by written notice as set out in the agreement. You keep every account and file.

      Who is this for?

      Local businesses that serve customers in person or nearby: salons and barbers, dental and wellness clinics, trades and home services, gyms and studios, shops, restaurants and cafés, and service pros. If your business is different, tell us. If a plan does not fit, we will say so.

      Are you local or remote?

      Most of the work is remote and runs on a weekly rhythm. Photo days and the Signature quarterly on-site review need the director in person, so distance matters for those: tell us where you are and we will say plainly what we can cover.
