Before you spend a credit

Straight answers

What does it cost, exactly?+

Credits are priced per second of video: two per second at 480p, four at 720p, more at 1080p and 4K — and the precise total displays before you commit. Plans start at $9.90/month with every model included, plus a free credit to claim daily. Smart move: test your idea on a 30-second excerpt at 480p, then render the full song once.

Can I sell what I make?+

Yes — every plan includes a commercial license covering social content, YouTube, ads, client work, and paid media. Paid plans export watermark-free.

What if a render fails?+

Credits return to your account automatically. You only ever pay for finished videos.

Do I need editing skills?+

No timeline editor exists anywhere in the flow. Upload, describe, generate. The craft lives in how you describe the scene — which is exactly what the free Director's Guide teaches.

How fast is it?+

Short clips land in about three minutes. Full music videos process at roughly three to six minutes per minute of audio — start a render, make coffee, come back to a finished video.
