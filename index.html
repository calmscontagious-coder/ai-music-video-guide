Quick answers

What does it cost, exactly?

Credits are priced per second of video: 2/second at 480p, 4/second at 720p, more at 1080p/4K — and the precise total displays before you commit. Plans start at $9.90/month, every model included, plus a free credit to claim daily. Smart move: test your idea on a 30-second excerpt at 480p, then render the full song once.

Can I sell what I make?

Yes — every plan includes a commercial license covering social content, YouTube, ads, client work, and paid media. Paid plans export watermark-free.

What if a render fails?

Credits return to your account automatically. You only ever pay for finished videos.

Do I need editing skills?

No timeline editor exists anywhere in the flow. Upload, describe, generate. The craft lives in how you describe the scene — which is exactly what the free Director's Guide teaches.

How fast is it?

Short clips land in about 3 minutes. Full music videos process at roughly 3–6 minutes per minute of audio — start a render, make coffee, come back to a finished video.
