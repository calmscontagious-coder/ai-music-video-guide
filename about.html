About

A creative studio for local businesses

Purpose with Presence is an independent artist’s creative studio: web design, marketing, working tools and art. We run the online side of local businesses, so the people who own them can run the business in person.

What we make

  • Web design. Fast sites that look like the business they belong to and book or sell in a few taps on a phone.
  • Marketing. Google profiles kept complete, emails that bring people back, review replies, social posts and local ads, run every month.
  • Tools. Small working instruments a business can use: calculators, finders, checkers and booking pages.
  • Art. Music, drawing and writing. The habit of listening, cutting and redrawing until a thing sits right is the one we bring to every page.

How we work

A director, a person, runs each account and signs off every piece of work. Every account stays in the owner’s name and on the owner’s card. The owner says yes before anything goes live, and every price is published on the studio page.

Who runs it

Purpose with Presence is operated by an individual sole proprietor. We also publish free guides for musicians making videos with AI; they are reader-supported through affiliate links, explained in our affiliate disclosure.
