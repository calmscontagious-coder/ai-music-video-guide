About

An honest guide to AI video

AI Music Video Guide is an independent publication for musicians and creators who want to make professional videos with AI — without a film crew, an editor, or a huge budget.

The AI video space moves fast and most of what's written about it is either hype or thinly-disguised advertising. We take a different approach: we actually use these tools, document what works, and publish practical, specific guidance you can act on today. Our flagship resource, the Director's Guide, is the exact playbook we use to direct renders that look intentional instead of generic.

What we cover

  • Tutorials — step-by-step guides for turning a song into a full music video, getting the most from each engine, and shipping to every platform.
  • Honest comparisons — clear-eyed breakdowns of the major AI video models (Seedance, Sora 2, Veo 3 and more) so you can pick the right one.
  • Craft — prompt engineering, credit strategy, and the art-direction choices that separate a real video from AI slop.

How we stay free

Everything here is free to read. We keep it that way through affiliate partnerships: when you sign up for a tool through our links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. It never changes what we recommend — we only point you toward tools we'd use ourselves. See our full affiliate disclosure.
