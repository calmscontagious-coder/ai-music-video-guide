His images: what is in, what is out.

Every image his live site serves was fetched on 3 November 2026 from the 54 pages in his sitemap: 123 files, about 70 distinct photographs. On the demo now: 61 photographs, his 3 art boards and his 2 videos, each image as AVIF and WebP with a JPEG fallback, lazy below the first screen. The videos went from 7.2 Mbps 1080p to about 1 MB each and load only after the page has painted. Left out, each with its reason:

  • IMG_7476.jpg: the hand-mixer photograph, retired by the operator (HOME_BLEND 2026-10-22, standard 4.2)
  • il_1140xN.2930324703_njqz.webp: cookies printed with Star Wars characters: third-party character art we cannot publish on our demo
  • il_1140xN.2882676284_38m0.webp: heart cookies personalised with a private person’s name and a date
  • Gemini_Generated_Image_b5by1mb5by1mb5by.png: an AI-generated banner (its own file name); canon 4.1 bars AI imagery
