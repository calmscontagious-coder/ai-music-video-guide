What the merch is made of.

Everything in the pastry case is made without animal products, and the merch follows the same rule: no leather, wool, silk, feathers, beeswax, carmine or bone china, down to the thread, the ink, the label and the glue. Before a piece goes on sale, its maker confirms in writing that every part of it is free of animal materials and was never tested on animals.

Printed to order, so little sits in a stockroom.

How the line runs once the shop says yes. Costs are supplier base prices before shipping, read 22 October 2026.

  1. One master file per line

    Fraunces with the type turned to outlines and the heart on its own red layer. A native French speaker reads every line and every accent before anything prints.

  2. Printful connects to Shopify

    Free plan, or Growth at $24.99 a month, free once sales pass $12,000 a year, with 21 integrations including Shopify and TikTok Shop. Printify is the alternative: free, Premium at $39 a month, 11 integrations, its TikTok link US only.

  3. An order prints itself

    The guest checks out on id-eclair.com and Printful prints, packs and ships. The tins, pins, counter mugs, screen-printed totes and the wine key are small runs the shop keeps behind the counter.

  4. TikTok Shop reads from Shopify

    Printful connects to Shopify only, since a second direct TikTok connector can break order status. Merchandise opens the shop’s TikTok Shop; the plan prices the referral fee at 8% until Seller Center confirms it.

Questions about the merch.

The short answer first.

Can I buy iD Éclair merch online today?

Three things, yes: cookie spoons at $5, the custom printed cookie at $3.75 each and the Golden Ticket class pass at $140, all on id-eclair.com. The rest of this page is a planned line with proposed prices, waiting for the shop’s approval.

Is the merch vegan?

Yes, by the same rule as the pastry case: no animal materials in any part of a piece, from the fabric to the glue, confirmed in writing by each maker before it goes on sale. The plan keeps those statements on file and lists the materials on every product page.

Which pieces are collectibles?

The numbered ones planned for the first season: La Boîte Cœur macaron tins, edition of 150 for Noël 2026 and 100 for Saint-Valentin 2027, the signed Nuancier print, edition of 100, and the alumni pin from the Macaron Masterclass.
