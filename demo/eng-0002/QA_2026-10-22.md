## 2. What was fixed

### Same header, app bar and footer on every page
- **index.html** linked its header, footer and app bar only to its own sections. **menu.html** sent Cakes, Classes, La Cave and Chef Romy to sections on the home page, even though each one has its own page. All seven pages now share one header, one footer and one app bar, built from a single template. Only `aria-current` and the same-page anchors change from page to page (`#box` and `#pickup` on the home page, `#menu` and `#order` on the menu page).
- Nav targets everywhere: Menu `menu.html`, Macarons `index.html#box`, Cakes `cakes.html`, Classes `classes.html`, La Cave `la-cave.html`, Chef Romy `story.html`. Order for pickup goes to `menu.html`, or to `#menu` on the menu page itself.
- The footer's "Gifts that ship" link went to the home page tiles on six pages and to an id that does not exist on the seventh. It now opens `menu.html#ships`, which the menu page reads as its Ships filter.
- The Bag tab on cakes.html and classes.html was missing `data-section="pickup"`. Every page now has it.
- The bag pill moved inside the footer, and the concept notice changed from a `div` to `<aside aria-label="Concept preview">`. Every element on every page now sits inside a landmark.
- Cart badge, bag pill and pickup time were tested in the browser. An item added on the home page showed as 2 items with the same pickup time on all six other pages. Checkout built the Shopify cart link for the live-store item only, and Escape closed the drawer and put focus back.

### No pink
- The header and footer showed `logo.png`, which carries the large blush ghost "Da" behind the wordmark. That was the most visible pink on every page. Both now use `img/logo-wordmark.png`: the client's own artwork, with the navy wordmark, the red heart and the tagline, and the ghost letters removed. This is a concept variant, and the client needs to approve it (see section 5). `logo.png` is still in the folder, so going back takes one line in the template.
- Searched every page, the CSS and the JS. No blush or pink colour remains. The only pink-family colour is the raspberry macaron swatch, `--flav-raspberry: #b8325a`, a 14 px dot for a real flavour. It was kept, and is noted in section 4.

### Links
- index.html: "Start a cake request" linked to its own section; it now goes to `cakes.html`. "Reserve a seat" now goes to `classes.html`. The three tiles under "Next door, and in the mail" linked to their own section; they now go to `la-cave.html`, `menu.html#ships` and `merch.html`. A "Read Chef Romy's story" link to `story.html` was added under the chef section.
- menu.html: "See the class", "Start a cake request", "Visit La Cave" and the link in the cakes group note now go to their own pages instead of home-page sections.
- Checker: `site_audit/check_links.py` checks every href, src, srcset and data-image, plus image paths inside inline scripts, `app.js` and the JSON files, and every #fragment against the ids on the target page. Final run: **405 references, 0 broken**. External links are listed but not fetched.

### Higher-resolution photos
- index.html still used the 1200 px files from the first demo, with no srcset (macarons, cannelés, the four jars, truffles, cheese board, gift basket, Golden Ticket). It now uses the 1600 px upscaled set with 800 px fallbacks and srcset, like the other pages (manifest: Real-ESRGAN 4x on sources narrower than 1600 px).
- The Merchandise tile showed `golden-ticket.jpg`, a mockup with ticket text baked into the image, which the image manifest already excludes. It now shows the cookie spoon photo, a merchandise item on the live store today. The tile copy now matches merch.html: the tea tin and the pin are small runs, not print on demand.
- The chef section on the home page reused the cannelé photo. It now shows the chef at the pastry competition. The box builder now shows the six-macaron box photo.
- Each product now has the same bag thumbnail on every page. The Golden Ticket uses its own product photo instead of the baked-text mockup, and the assorted box, truffles and jars use the upscaled files.
- menu.html's map of local images gained the two wholesale products. They do not show today; the entry stops a `.heic` file from the store's CDN, which Chrome cannot display, from appearing if that category is turned on.

### Accessibility
- **Contrast.** Small text on the classes page's Full and Sales-closed ticket stubs measured 3.62:1 and 2.73:1. The stubs were restyled and now measure 5.36:1 and 4.96:1.
- **Contrast.** The home page tile captions sat on a gradient that left the heading over about 24% indigo on bright photos, roughly 1.6:1. The gradient now gives 80 to 92% under the text, at least 6:1.
- Token pairs, measured: ink on ivory 16.23:1, ink on white 17.34:1, indigo on ivory 14.26:1, ivory on indigo 14.26:1, white on indigo 15.24:1, muted on ivory 6.12:1, quiet indigo on deep ivory 4.96:1, white on red badge 4.75:1. Red error text appears only on white (4.75:1). Red on ivory would be 4.45:1, so it is not used there for text.
- `aria-label` on generic elements (the box slots, the home page seat dots, the La Cave seat dots) now has a role (`group` or `img`).
- Footer links were 21 px tall and are now 27 px. The "My dates are flexible" checkbox on the classes page went from 20 to 24 px. Every other target was already at least 24 px; links inside sentences are exempt under WCAG 2.5.8.
- The home page seat dots now carry a visible "Example seats" label.
- Static checker `site_audit/check_a11y.py`: one H1 on each page; header, nav, main and footer on each page; every input, select and textarea has a label; every image has alt text; every button and link has a name; no duplicate ids; no broken aria references; every `target="_blank"` link has `rel="noopener"`.
- Focus: the global 3 px red ring measures 3.3:1 on indigo and 4.45:1 on ivory. Hidden radio and checkbox inputs show the ring on their labels.

### Phone first
- `.row` (price plus Add button) did not wrap. With an item in the bag, the − 1 + stepper ran 3 px past the card at 360 px and 13 to 23 px past it at 320 px, where it was clipped. This was measured in Chrome. Rows now wrap.
- Cards (`.item`, `.ck-tile`) now use a single `minmax(0,1fr)` column. The word "Anniversary" in the cake gallery and the printed-cookie style rows on merch had been forcing cards wider than their grid cells at 320 and 360 px.
- The floating pickup chip now has a maximum width and wraps. A long label such as "Next pickup Thu Oct 23, 10:00 am" could run past the photo.
- The merch numbers table scrolls inside its own box if it ever gets too wide.
- Measured in Chrome at 375, 360 and 320 px, with every Add button pressed (menu in both list and photo view): page width equals viewport width on all 7 pages, and no button, price or heading is clipped inside a card. At 768, 900, 1000 and 1280 px there is no horizontal scroll and the header fits on one line. No console errors on any page while the box builder, cake steps, ticket choice, table size and merch buttons were used.
- Inputs are all 16 px or larger, so iOS does not zoom when a field is focused.

### Honesty
- Follower counts now carry their date: Instagram 42.8K on the home page and La Cave 10.6K on the La Cave page, both read 21 October 2026.
- The home page's cake ladder said "9-inch serves 10 to 14", which the cakes page does not offer. It now matches the cakes page and is labelled as example sizes: 6-inch serves 4 to 6, 8-inch 7 to 12, 10-inch 13 to 20.
- Every page still has the slim Concept preview notice with a dated source line. Pickup slots, seat counts, table booking, the Saturday drop and shipping badges are all labelled Example. There are no customer testimonials. The quotes on the story page are the shop's own wording, each with its source.

- **menu.json image fields** still point at the 1200 px files from the first demo for nine products. menu.html swaps in the upscaled files when it renders, so nothing shows wrong today. Update `site_audit/build_menu_json.py` to write the upscaled names, so any other page that renders from the file gets them too.
- **Raspberry swatch** (#b8325a, hue 342) on the box builder. It stands for a real flavour and is a small dot, not a background tint. Deepen it if the operator wants zero pink-family colour.
- **Hero buttons** on index.html and classes.html pair a filled button with a ghost button (see the gate table). It is a design call, not a failure.
- **Fonts** load from Google Fonts. The type falls back to Didot or Times and the system sans if they are blocked.
- **Independence.** This pass and the gate are static rules plus browser measurements by the same model family that built the pages. A cross-model grade (local qwen2.5:14b) has not been run. Count the result as `is_independent: false`.
- Files the macOS drive writes, named `._*`, sit beside every file. Leave them out of any upload.
