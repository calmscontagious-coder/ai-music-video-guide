Your new site for 169 W Green St, ready to choose.

Everything the studio has built for the shop sits on this one page. Start with the recommended home page, look at the eleven directions it grew from, then answer three questions. Open each link on your phone: that is how most customers will meet it.

1The recommended direction

The home blend: Salon’s story, Carte’s order strip.

One home page made from the two strongest directions below.

Why this one

  1. The story sells as it goes. The afternoon on Green Street is told in chapters, from la vitrine to l’invitation, and every chapter closes on something to add or book.
  2. Six taps to checkout, from anywhere. Add two pastries, a box of six and pay in 6 taps with the earliest slot kept. Pastries and jars from the live store reach the Shopify cart in 3 taps, one fewer than before.
  3. The pickup time never hides. A Pickup, Add, Pay strip follows you down the page and now rides on every inner page too. The slot picker opens 1.3 phone screens down instead of 5.2, though the box of six still sits about 6 screens down the home page.

Measured on a 375 px phone screen in a test browser on 22 October 2026. Counter items still pay at the shop until the pickup app joins checkout.

6How to give feedback

Three questions for the chef.

Short answers are fine. A word per question is enough to start the next round.

  1. Which direction?The home blend, or one of the eleven. If a mood above feels more like the shop, say which.
  2. What should it keep from the others?A headline, a photo, a section, the way one of them sells gifts or classes.
  3. What is missing?A product, a fact about the shop, a question customers ask at the counter, something that feels wrong.

Send your answers to the studio by text or email, whichever is easier. A voice note or a photo of a sketch works too.

There is no form on this page, on purpose: nothing you type here is stored or sent.
