Questions about gift cards.

The short answer first.

Can it be used at the counter?

Yes. One card, one balance, at the counter at 169 W Green St, in the salon de thé and on the website. The counter scans the code from the email.

Does it expire?

No. California does not allow an expiry date or a service fee on a gift card, and a card with less than $10 left can be cashed out at the counter on request.

Can I send it later?

Yes. Choose a date and it goes that morning, with your line. Leave the date empty and it goes the minute the order is paid.
