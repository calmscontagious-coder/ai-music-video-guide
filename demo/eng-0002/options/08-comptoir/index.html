Answers first

Is everything plant-based?

Yes. Every pastry, cake, jar and drink in the shop is 100% plant-based, croissants included.

Can I order tonight and pick up tomorrow?

Yes. Choose tomorrow in the pickup picker and a time; same-day slots start soon after you order.

What checks out on id-eclair.com today?

Items tagged Live store, such as the cannelés by three, the love-note jars, truffles and the Golden Ticket, go through the shop’s own Shopify checkout. Counter items join that checkout when the pickup app arrives; today they are ordered at the counter or on the menu app.

How far ahead do I order a custom cake?

Seven days, which is the shop’s own rule.

Where do I park?

The De Lacey garage at 45 S. De Lacey Ave is a two-minute walk; the first two hours cost $1.00.
