From the bench to your hands in four steps.

One cart for pastries, cakes, seats, gifts and merchandise, paid on Shopify. Today, items marked On id-eclair.com today open the live store’s checkout, where pickup is usually ready in 24 hours; the rest join the same checkout when the pickup app goes live.

  1. Choose the moment

    A day, then a 15-minute slot with room left. The earliest is chosen for you; anything that can’t be ready by then shows its notice.

  2. Pay in two taps

    Apple Pay, Google Pay or Shop Pay, as a guest if you like. The total you saw is the total you pay.

  3. Thank the team, if you wish

    One optional line on the payment step, after the slot is set, with nothing preselected.

  4. Say your name at the counter

    A text when your box is on the shelf, if you opted in. Your name and slot are on the label.

Questions, answered first.

Is everything plant-based?

Yes, all of it. Every pastry, macaron, cake and drink is made from plants: plant butter in the croissants, plant milk in the latte.

Can I order éclairs ahead for a set time?

Yes, by day and 15-minute slot. Today éclairs are ordered at the counter or on the shop’s menu app; in the new checkout they sit in the same cart as everything else.

How much notice does a custom cake need?

One week. For a wedding, ask early: the chef answers with the dates he can take.

When is the Macaron Masterclass?

Tuesdays, 3:30 to 5:30 pm, $125.00 a seat. You leave with 20 to 30 macarons. Seats are very limited, and the Golden Ticket at $140.00 is the gift version.

Where do I pick up my order?

At the counter, 169 W Green St, Old Pasadena. Give your name; the box is labelled with it and your slot. La Cave is next door at 163 W Green St.

What can be shipped?

Shelf-stable gifts and merchandise. Jams, truffles, cookies and the planned clothing line are the planned shipping range; éclairs and cream pastries stay pickup only.

Does the tip go to the staff?

Yes, all of it. The optional tip at checkout goes to the bakers and counter team who make and pack the order, as California Labor Code 351 requires.

When is La Cave open?

Wednesday to Sunday, 3 to 9 pm, according to La Cave’s own site. It pours organic, biodynamic and vegan wines next door at 163 W Green St.
