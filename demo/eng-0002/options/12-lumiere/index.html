Is everything plant-based?

Yes. Everything in the case is made from plants, with no eggs and no dairy. The croissants are laminated with plant butter.

What about allergies?

Many pieces carry nuts or coconut, such as the almond streusel in the jars, and wheat appears in many recipes. Tell the counter about any allergy before you order.

How far ahead should I order a celebration cake?

A week. The shop asks for a week’s notice on custom cakes, and the chef replies with a sketch and a price before anything is baked.

When is the Macaron Masterclass?

Tuesdays, 3:30 to 5:30 pm, at $125.00 a seat, with 20 to 30 macarons to take home. Seats are very limited. Today they’re booked by DM or in person.

What is La Cave?

The vegan wine bar one door down, at 163 W Green St: organic, biodynamic and vegan wines, Rebel Cheese boards and plated desserts from the pâtisserie. Its published hours are Wednesday to Sunday, 3 to 9 pm, for guests 21 and over.
