Questions, answered first.

Where can I get vegan éclairs in Pasadena?

At 169 W Green St in Old Pasadena, where every pastry in the case is plant-based. The chocolate and pistachio éclairs are $6.50, the espresso éclair $5.50 and the chef’s special $9, from the shop’s menu app on 21 October 2026.

Is everything plant-based, egg-free and dairy-free?

Yes. The whole case is made from plants, with no eggs and no dairy; the croissants are laminated with plant butter. Almond and wheat appear in many recipes, so note any allergy at the counter or in the cake request.

Can I order tonight and pick up tomorrow?

Yes, in the new checkout: choose tomorrow, then a 15-minute slot, and pay. Next-day orders stay open overnight, as proposed in the plan. On the live store today, pickup is usually ready in 24 hours.

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

One week, which is the shop’s own rule. Weddings start with a consultation, proposed at four weeks ahead.

Are there macaron classes in Pasadena?

Yes. The Macaron Masterclass with Chef Romy runs on Tuesdays from 3:30 to 5:30 pm at 169 W Green St, $125 a seat, with 20 to 30 macarons to take home. The Golden Ticket, $140, gives it as a present.

What is La Cave?

The vegan wine bar next door, at 163 W Green St. It opens Wednesday to Sunday from 3 to 9 pm, with organic, biodynamic and vegan wines, Rebel Cheese boards and plated desserts from the pâtisserie.

Can you send gifts to one person or a whole office?

Yes. Coffrets and the Golden Ticket check out on id-eclair.com today for pickup, and offices write to the concierge on this page. Shipping starts with merchandise; jams, truffles and biscuits follow once the food paperwork is approved.
