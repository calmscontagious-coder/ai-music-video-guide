II

Le chefRomuald Guiot, a fourth-generation pastry chef

He cooked at La Maison de Marc Veyrat, three Michelin stars, moved to the United States in 2015 and opened La Mirabelle in New York, then worked at Pitchoun and at Porto’s. On season 8 of Food Network’s Spring Baking Championship he reached the final.

Green Street opened in December 2024 with every recipe rebuilt from plants. The chef told Flaunt that guests are often surprised to learn it.

Questions, answered first

Is everything plant-based?
Yes. Every pastry, cake and drink at iD Éclair is made from plants, and La Cave next door is a vegan wine bar.
Can I choose my pickup time?
Yes, in the new checkout: you pick a day, then a time. Same-day pickup starts 30 minutes after you order (proposed). The live store today says orders are usually ready in 24 hours.
How much notice does a custom cake need?
One week, the shop’s own rule. Whole menu cakes need 48 hours and wedding cakes four weeks (both proposed).
When is the Macaron Masterclass?
Tuesdays, 3:30 to 5:30 pm, at $125 a seat, with 20 to 30 macarons to take home. Seats are very limited; the Golden Ticket ($140) gives a seat as a present.
Is La Cave part of iD Éclair?
Yes. La Cave is the same chef’s vegan wine bar, next door at 163 W Green St, open Wednesday to Sunday from 3 to 9 pm as published on its own site.
Is there a fee for ordering online?
No. The price on the menu is the price at checkout. The one extra line is an optional tip, and every tip goes to the bakers and counter team.
