Quand passez-vous ?

Order from the line. Pick up past it.

Choose the day and the slot before you leave home, or while you wait for the light on Green Street. Your name goes on the box.

  1. Fill the bag

    The box of six, an éclair, a jar. Live-store items keep their real stock, so a sold-out piece says so instead of taking the order.

  2. Choose a day and a slot

    The picker opens on the earliest slot. Cakes carry their own notice, so the picker hides times the kitchen can’t make.

  3. Say your name at the counter

    Staff tap Ready for pickup, you get the note, and a QR slip sits on your box.

How checkout works

  • Live-store items check out on id-eclair.com today: the bag opens a Shopify cart with your items already in it.
  • Counter items, like the éclairs, are ordered at the counter or on the menu app today.
  • At checkout: guest checkout with Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay, and the total you see is the total. No fee appears at the end.

About tipping. The live checkout shows an optional tip line. Leave it at zero or add what you like; nothing is added for you.

Prefer delivery? The shop is on Uber Eats, DoorDash and Grubhub, at those apps’ prices.

Asked in line, answered first.

Where can I get vegan éclairs in Pasadena?

At 169 W Green St in Old Pasadena, where every éclair is plant-based. Chocolate and pistachio are $6.50, espresso $5.50 and the chef’s special $9.

Is everything plant-based?

Yes. Everything in the case is made from plants, with no eggs and no dairy. The croissants are laminated with plant butter. Many pieces carry nuts or coconut, such as the almond streusel in the jars, so tell the counter about any allergy before you order.

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

A week. The shop asks for a week’s notice on custom cakes. For a wedding, ask early: the chef answers with the dates he can take.

Can I order tonight and pick up tomorrow?

Yes: you choose the day and a 15-minute slot in the bag. Today, the live store’s pickup is usually ready in 24 hours, and counter orders go through the menu app.

When is the Macaron Masterclass?

Tuesdays, 3:30 to 5:30 pm, at $125 a seat, with 20 to 30 macarons to take home. Seats are very limited. Today they’re booked by DM or in person; the Golden Ticket ($140) is the gift version.

What is La Cave?

The vegan wine bar one door down, at 163 W Green St. Organic, biodynamic and vegan wines, Rebel Cheese boards and plated desserts from the pâtisserie. Its published hours are Wednesday to Sunday, 3 to 9 pm, for guests 21 and over.

Is there a TikTok Shop?

It opens with the merchandise line first. Shelf-stable sweets follow only once the food paperwork is in place, and fresh pastry never lists there. Follow @ideclairlosangeles for the opening.
