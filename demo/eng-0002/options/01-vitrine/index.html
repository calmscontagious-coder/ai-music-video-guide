Questions, answered first.

The answers a phone customer needs before the first tap, in the words the counter uses.

Is everything plant-based?

Yes. Every pastry, cake and drink in the case is made from plants, down to the plant butter laminated into the croissants. The shop’s store lists the macarons and cannelés as gluten-free; for anything else, ask at the counter.

How far ahead do I order?

Counter pastries the same day, menu cakes 48 hours ahead, designed cakes a week ahead. The earliest same-day pickup proposed is 30 minutes after ordering, with slots open up to 14 days out. Wedding cakes take four weeks (proposed).

What does checkout add to the price?

Nothing you did not choose. No service fee appears at the end. The one optional line is a thank-you tip for the bakers and counter team, 10, 15 or 20 percent or your own amount, with nothing preselected.

Where do I pick up?

At the counter, 169 W Green St, Old Pasadena. Say your name; the box is shelved by pickup time. Parking nearby is paid, so a set slot keeps the stop short.

Do you ship?

Not yet. Today every order is picked up at 169 W Green St. The plan ships only what travels: truffles, jams, biscuits and the merchandise line. Éclairs and cream pastries stay pickup only.

When are you open?

The case opens at 6 am; the shop’s About page lists Mondays as closed. Closing times differ between the shop’s listings today, so this preview leaves them out; the new site shows one confirmed set everywhere. La Cave next door opens Wednesday to Sunday, 3 to 9 pm.
