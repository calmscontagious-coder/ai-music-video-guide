    How the shop changes its look

    One setting, and a calendar the shop can edit.

    1. In Shopify, Online Store, Themes, Customize, Theme settings, Skin. Leave it on Auto and the theme follows the calendar.
    2. The calendar is a Shopify metaobject with the same fields as skins/calendar.json: dates, colours, hero line and the product to feature. Adding a skin is a new entry, not new code.
    3. To dress the shop for one day only, choose a named skin, then set Skin back to Auto. Auto returns to La maison the moment a window ends.
    4. A skin changes colour, the hairline, heading weight and the motif. The page, the prices, the bag and checkout stay exactly as they are.

    Answers first

    Does a skin change prices or checkout?

    No. A skin moves colours, the hairline, heading weight and the motif. Every price, the bag, the pickup picker and the Shopify checkout are the same in all 25.

    How does the shop switch the look?

    One theme setting, Skin, set to Auto or to a named skin. Auto follows the calendar and returns to La maison by itself when a window ends.

    Is everything plant-based?

    Yes. Every pastry, cake, jar and drink in the shop is 100% plant-based, in every season.

    Which dates are not confirmed yet?

    ArtNight Pasadena has not announced its 2027 nights; the calendar marks them to confirm. Every other 2026 and 2027 date was checked against a source opened on 24 October 2026.
