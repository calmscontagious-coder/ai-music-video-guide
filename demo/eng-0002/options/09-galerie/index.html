Notes on the catalogue

Is everything plant-based?

Yes. Every pastry, cake, jar and drink in the shop is 100% plant-based, the croissant included.

Why one piece to a page?

Because the case is the shop’s finest room. Every plate has its price and an Add button, and Pay joins the pickup strip once something is in the bag.

Which pieces check out on id-eclair.com today?

Plates marked Live store, such as the cannelés, the jars, the truffles and the cheese board, go through the shop’s Shopify checkout. The éclair, the croissant, the box of six and the class join it with the pickup app; today they are ordered at the counter or on the menu app.

How far ahead do I order a custom cake?

Seven days, which is the shop’s own rule. The cakes page has sizes and flavours.
