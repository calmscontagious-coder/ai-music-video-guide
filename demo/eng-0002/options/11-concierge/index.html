        VIIHow this works

        What runs in your browser today, and what the real store adds.

        The concierge is built so the honest version and the clever version give the same answers. The rules come first everywhere; a language model only ever fills the gaps, and only from the same two files.

        In your browser, today

        1. Two files load. menu.json, 82 rows with prices, gluten marks and lead notes, and facts.json, 29 facts that each name the research file they came from.
        2. Rules read your question. Words such as gluten, class or cake pick the answer; item names pick the row. Voice, where the browser has it, only types the words for you.
        3. The answer shows its source. Under every reply: the file, the row, the date it was read, and whether a fact is proposed or an example. No match means “the shop can tell you”.
        4. Actions write to the one bag. Add, the box of six, the slot picker and the cake request share the bag every page of the preview uses.
        5. Pay is a Shopify cart link. Live-store items open id-eclair.com/cart with their variant ids, as real orders.

        In the real store, through the worker

        1. Same address, signed. The page posts to /apps/concierge on the shop’s domain; Shopify’s app proxy signs it and passes it to a Cloudflare Worker.
        2. Rules first, again. The worker runs this same engine. Most questions end here and cost nothing.
        3. The model fills gaps only. For the rest it sends the matching rows, never the whole menu, to a language model through the VAEL gateway or Claude, with orders to quote nothing else.
        4. Every reply is checked. Each price, time and item in the reply must appear in those rows, or the visitor gets “the shop can tell you” instead.
        5. Counts, not words. The worker logs how many questions, which kind and how fast, never the question, and allows 20 a minute from one visitor.

        Written and tested, not deployed: deploying needs the shop’s Cloudflare login.

        Questions about the concierge

        Answered here in full, the way the concierge answers: from what the shop has said.

        Is Le Concierge a chatbot?

        On this preview it is a rule engine: it matches your question to the shop’s menu file and a short facts file and answers only from them. In the real store, questions the rules miss go to a language model through a Cloudflare Worker, which may use only the matching rows and is checked before you see the answer.

        Does it keep what I type?

        No. On this preview your words stay in the browser tab. A usual you save sits in this browser’s local storage, and one tap forgets it. Voice input uses your browser’s speech service; in Chrome that sends the audio to Google.

        How much notice does a custom cake need?

        One week, the shop’s own rule. Whole menu cakes need 48 hours and wedding cakes four weeks (both proposed). The composer counts the date for you.

        Is anything gluten-free?

        The counter board marks the soup du jour, and the endive and grandmother beet salades without their crostini. The live store labels the assorted macarons, cannelés by 3, the four dessert jars and several chocolates gluten free. Ask the counter about cross-contact if it is for coeliac disease.

        Can I pay here?

        Live-store items check out on id-eclair.com through a Shopify cart link, as real orders. Counter items and cake requests wait in the bag for the new pickup checkout.
