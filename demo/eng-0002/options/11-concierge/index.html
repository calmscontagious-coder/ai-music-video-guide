    VIIHow it answers

    From the shop’s own menu, with the source under every reply.

    Le Concierge answers only from what the shop has published. When it does not know, it says the shop can tell you.

    How it answers

    1. It reads the menu. Prices, gluten-free marks and lead times come from the shop’s menu and its published facts.
    2. It shows its source. Under every reply: where the answer came from and the date it was read, and whether a detail is an example.
    3. It fills one bag. Add, the box of six, the pickup time and the cake request share the bag every page uses.
    4. Pay opens the live store. Live-store items check out on id-eclair.com, as real orders.

    What it keeps

    1. Your words stay here. What you type stays in this browser tab.
    2. A usual stays on this device. Save one and it is here next time, and nowhere else.
    3. No guessing. A question with no answer in the menu gets “the shop can tell you”.

    Questions about the concierge

    Answered here in full, the way the concierge answers: from what the shop has said.

    Is Le Concierge a chatbot?

    It is the shop’s menu, answering: it matches your question to the menu and the shop’s published facts, answers only from them and shows where each answer came from.

    Does it keep what I type?

    No. Your words stay on this page. A usual you save stays on this phone, and one tap forgets it. Voice input uses your browser’s speech service; in Chrome that sends the audio to Google.

    How much notice does a custom cake need?

    One week, the shop’s own rule. For a whole menu cake or a wedding, ask the shop for its dates. The composer counts the date for you.

    Is anything gluten-free?

    The counter board marks the soup du jour, and the endive and grandmother beet salades without their crostini. The live store labels the assorted macarons, cannelés by 3, the four dessert jars and several chocolates gluten free. Ask the counter about cross-contact if it is for coeliac disease.

    Can I pay here?

    Live-store items check out on id-eclair.com, as real orders. Counter items and cake requests wait in the bag for the new pickup checkout.
