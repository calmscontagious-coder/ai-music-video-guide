Answers first.

What people ask search engines and assistants about Green Street, answered in two sentences each.

  • Where can I get vegan éclairs in Pasadena?

    At 169 W Green St in Old Pasadena, where every éclair in the case is plant-based. On the counter menu, read 21 October 2026, chocolate and pistachio are $6.50, espresso $5.50 and the chef’s special $9.

  • Is everything plant-based, with no eggs or dairy?

    Yes. The croissants are laminated with plant butter, and the éclairs, macarons and cakes contain no animal products, like everything else in the vitrine. The full allergen list goes on each product with the new menu.

  • Can I order tonight and choose a pickup time for tomorrow?

    In the new checkout, yes: pick the day, then a 15-minute slot, and next-day orders stay open overnight (proposed). Until then, scheduled pickup runs on the shop’s menu app.

  • How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

    At least a week, the shop’s own rule. Whole menu cakes need 48 hours, and wedding cakes about four weeks (both proposed in the plan).

  • Are there macaron classes in Pasadena?

    Yes. The Macaron Masterclass with Chef Romy runs on Tuesdays from 3:30 to 5:30 pm at 169 W Green St, $125 a seat, and you take home 20 to 30 macarons.

  • Do you serve afternoon tea?

    Yes, in the salon de thé: small pastries and savory bites with Palais des Thés teas, also listed as Tea Time for Two. The planner on this page shows how a table will book.

  • What is La Cave?

    The vegan wine bar next door at 163 W Green St: organic, biodynamic and vegan wines, Rebel Cheese boards and plated desserts from the pâtisserie, Wednesday to Sunday, 3 to 9 pm.

  • Can I send a gift to one person or a whole office?

    Gift baskets ($45 and $55), truffles, jams and the four-jar discovery box ($30) are on the live store today, and the Golden Ticket ($140) turns a class into a present. Office orders get their own corporate gifts page in the build.
