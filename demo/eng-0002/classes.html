  • Each guest’s name, so every seat carries a name.

Questions about the masterclass.

The short answer first.

Is there a macaron class in Pasadena?

Yes. Chef Romy teaches a hands-on Macaron Masterclass at 169 W Green St in Old Pasadena on Tuesdays from 3:30 to 5:30 pm, at $125 a seat. Seats are very limited.

What do I take home from the masterclass?

Between 20 and 30 macarons, boxed, made with the same plant-based recipes as the vitrine. To taste them first, add a box of six to a pickup order.

How do I book a seat?

Today the shop books classes by Instagram message or in person. The new site sells seats on a calendar with a waitlist; on this preview, Reserve puts an example seat in your bag.

Can I give the class as a gift?

Yes. The Golden Ticket, $140 on id-eclair.com, is a pass to an upcoming class with an organic chocolate tablet, and the date is arranged with the shop. A boxed version is part of the planned merchandise line.
