Questions about ordering for a group.

Short answers; the request form settles the rest.

How much notice does the kitchen need?

The board for ten and the truffle box are on the live store today and go in the bag for your pickup time. Trays, printed cookies and anything for more than about twenty people start with the request; custom cakes need a week, the shop’s own rule.

Is everything vegan?

Yes. Everything from the case is vegan, the first French vegan pastry shop in Los Angeles, and the boards use Rebel Cheese. Say the allergies in the request and the kitchen marks the trays.

Do you deliver?

Pickup at 169 W Green St is the standard. Delivery in Pasadena is quoted with the order; the shop confirms it in the reply.
