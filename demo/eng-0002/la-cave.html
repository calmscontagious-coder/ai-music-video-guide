Questions about La Cave.

The short answer first.

Is La Cave a vegan wine bar?

Yes. La Cave, next to the pâtisserie in Old Pasadena, pours organic, biodynamic and vegan wines, mostly French varietals from California and abroad, with Rebel Cheese boards, small plates and plated desserts from next door.

When is La Cave open?

Wednesday to Sunday, 3 to 9 pm, as La Cave and the Old Pasadena directory publish it. Wine is served to guests 21 and over.

Does La Cave take reservations?

Not yet; events, happy hour and Saturday nights are announced on its Instagram, @lacavepasadena.

Can I take the cheese board home?

Yes. The board for ten, $100.00 on id-eclair.com, is pickup only at 169 W Green St: Rebel Cheese, baguette, dried fruit and nuts, with a charcuterie version on request. For the morning after, order éclairs for pickup.
