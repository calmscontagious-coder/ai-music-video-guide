Choose the moment. It will be boxed for then.

The picker lives inside the Shopify checkout. It knows the kitchen’s prep time and closes a slot when the day is full.

  1. Add from the live counter

    Stock is the real stock. When the last Paris-Brest goes, the button says so instead of taking an order the kitchen cannot fill.

  2. Pick a day and a time

    Same-day slots open after the morning bake. Cakes and large orders carry their own lead time, so a Saturday cake cannot be booked for Saturday morning.

  3. Walk in, say your name

    A text when it is ready, your name on the box, the receipt already on your phone. The same order shows on the counter’s point of sale.
