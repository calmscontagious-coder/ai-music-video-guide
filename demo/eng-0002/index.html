IILes mains

Four generations of pastry, one pair of hands.

Romuald Guiot comes from a family of pastry cooks; the shop’s own story starts in a grandfather’s restaurant, with a blue apron. Chef Romy trained at La Maison de Marc Veyrat, opened La Mirabelle in New York, and reached the final of Food Network’s Spring Baking Championship.

On Green Street the technique stays French and the butter comes from plants. Guests are often surprised to learn that the whole case is vegan, the chef told Flaunt. After the Eaton Fire the shop gave éclairs away, and its daily leftovers now go to Friends In Deed in Pasadena.

IIILe rituel

Choose the moment. It is boxed for then.

Ordering ahead takes a minute on a phone: add from the case, pick a day and a time, and walk in to a box with your name on it.

  1. Add from the case

    Stock is the real stock. When the last Paris-Brest goes, the button says so instead of taking an order the kitchen cannot fill.

  2. Pick a day and a time

    Same-day slots open after the morning bake. Cakes carry their own notice, so a Saturday cake cannot be booked on Saturday morning.

  3. Say your name at the counter

    Your name on the box, a message when it is ready, the receipt already on your phone. Merci, à tout à l’heure.

IVLe salon de thé

Or stay a while, and take tea.

Afternoon tea is the third service on Green Street. The counter board lists thirteen Palais des Thés teas, espresso on plant milk, Romy’s hot chocolate, a French breakfast, the soup and sandwich du jour and four salades, and a tea party for two at $80 with the chef’s sweets and savories. The chalkboard sums the room up in three words of its own: Tasty, Fancy, and Frenchy! Reviewers single out the décor and the dishes, the outdoor tables, the welcome for dogs, and a staff that commits fully to the French theme.

Questions people ask about iD Éclair.

The short answer first, then the page that goes further.

Is there a vegan pâtisserie in Pasadena?

Yes. iD Éclair at 169 W Green St in Old Pasadena is a French pâtisserie and salon de thé where everything is made from plants: éclairs, croissants laminated with plant butter, macarons, cannelés, mousse cakes and tarts. See the menu or the chef’s story.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. You choose a day and a time and the order is boxed for then, to collect at the counter at 169 W Green St. Today the counter menu takes pickup orders on the shop’s menu app and packaged goods sell on id-eclair.com; the new order page puts both in one bag.

Does iD Éclair make custom vegan cakes?

Yes, with at least a week’s notice. Chef Romy makes custom cakes for birthdays, weddings and other occasions; today you start by calling, visiting or emailing the shop, and the cake page shows the six-step request planned for the new site.

Is there a macaron class in Pasadena?

Yes. The Macaron Masterclass with Chef Romy runs on Tuesdays from 3:30 to 5:30 pm at 169 W Green St, $125 a seat, and you take home 20 to 30 macarons. Seats are very limited; the class page has the dates and the Golden Ticket gift.

Is La Cave vegan too?

Yes. La Cave, next door at 163 W Green St, is a vegan wine bar pouring organic and biodynamic wines, with Rebel Cheese boards and plated desserts from the pâtisserie, Wednesday to Sunday from 3 to 9 pm. More on the La Cave page.
