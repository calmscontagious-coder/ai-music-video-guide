Pickup, built into the checkout

Pick a day. Pick a time. We box it for then.

The time picker lives inside the Shopify checkout, respects the kitchen’s prep time, and closes slots when the day is full.

  1. Choose from the live counter

    Stock is the real stock. When the last cannelés go, the button says so instead of taking an order we cannot fill.

  2. Choose a pickup day and time

    Same-day slots open after the chef’s prep window. Cakes and large orders get their own lead time, so a Saturday cake cannot be booked for Saturday morning.

  3. Collect at the counter

    A text when it is boxed, your name on the bag, and the receipt already in your phone. The same order shows on the shop’s point of sale.
