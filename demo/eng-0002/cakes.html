What Shopify does for cakes.

  • Menu-flavour cakes are ordinary products with sizes and a pickup date, so they share one cart with éclairs and boxes.
  • Sizes, flavours, the plaque and the photo uploads are product options (Globo Product Options in the plan, $9.90 a month).
  • Deposits and balances go out as draft-order invoices: a real Shopify checkout with Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay, at no software cost.
  • The pickup app in the plan (Bird) blocks any date inside the week, so a Saturday cake cannot be ordered on Friday.

Cake terms, written once.

Proposed in the plan, for the chef and counsel to confirm before launch:

  • A week’s notice for custom cakes; four weeks for wedding cakes.
  • A deposit of the greater of $50 or 25%, refundable more than a week out.
  • “Our interpretation, not a replica” for cakes ordered from photos.
  • No licensed characters or brand logos unless the owner has given permission.
  • An allergen note on every cake, written with the kitchen.

Questions about custom cakes.

The short answer first.

Does iD Éclair make custom vegan cakes in Pasadena?

Yes. Chef Romy makes custom plant-based cakes at 169 W Green St in Old Pasadena with at least a week’s notice. Today a request starts with a call, a visit or an email; the request on this page asks for the occasion, the guests, the flavour and up to three photos, and the chef replies with a sketch and a price.

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

At least one week. The shop states that rule today, and the calendar here starts seven days out. The plan proposes four weeks for wedding cakes, for the chef to confirm.

Can I send a photo of the cake I want?

Yes, up to three inspiration photos in step 5. The proposed terms say the chef makes his own interpretation rather than a replica, and licensed characters or brand logos need the owner’s permission.

Can I put a message on a cake?

Yes. Step 5 takes a message plaque of up to 35 characters, and in the new store the $15 individual mousse cake takes the same plaque at checkout. For a smaller treat, see today’s menu.
