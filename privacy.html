Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Purpose with Presence (“we”, “us”) runs this site and is responsible for the data described here. This policy says what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and who else handles it. The short version: no cookies, no ad trackers, no profiles, and we never sell or share personal information.

Information we collect

  • Email address — only if you subscribe to our newsletter or request the Director's Guide. We use MailerLite to manage subscriptions, with double opt-in: nothing is sent to you until you confirm by clicking the link in the confirmation email. MailerLite also records the date, the form you used and whether you open or click our emails.
  • Usage data — Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views, the referring site, country and device type. It sets no cookie, uses no local storage and does not fingerprint you; we see only totals. We do not sell this data.
  • Outbound click log — when you follow one of our paid links (addresses that start with /go/), our edge server records that a click happened: the link name, the page on this site you came from, your country, whether the device is a phone, tablet or desktop, and any campaign tag in the address. It does not record your IP address, a cookie, your browser identifier or anything that identifies you, and it sets nothing on your device. These counts are kept for 90 days and are used only to see which guides are useful.
  • Messages you send us — if you email us, we keep your message and address in our mailbox to answer you and follow up. Ask and we delete them.
  • Cookies and local storage — our own pages set no cookies. The home page loads Impact's tracking script (below), which can set its own cookie to attribute affiliate clicks. Your browser's local storage keeps the time you closed our sign-up bar or pop-up, so it stays closed, and MailerLite's form script stores a random visitor ID there (named ml_guid). On the client preview pages under /demo/, a menu chat assistant keeps your last messages in your browser's local storage so the conversation survives a reload; they are not sent anywhere. When you click a paid link, the merchant sets its own referral cookie on its site.

Calculators, quizzes and fitness tools

The calculators, planners and quizzes on this site, including those in the Fitness section, run entirely in your browser. What you type or select is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is gone when you leave the page. We do not collect consumer health data.

How we use it and our legal basis

  • Sending the guide and newsletter you asked for: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link.
  • Counting page views and paid-link clicks to improve our guides: our legitimate interest in knowing which pages help readers, using data that does not identify you.
  • Answering your emails: your request.

We never sell your personal information, never share it for advertising, and do not build profiles or make automated decisions about you.

How long we keep it

  • Subscriber data: until you unsubscribe. After that MailerLite keeps your address marked as unsubscribed so you are not emailed again; ask us and we erase it completely.
  • Paid-link click counts: 90 days.
  • Traffic statistics: aggregate totals only, kept by Cloudflare under its own retention.
  • Your emails to us: until the conversation is finished, or sooner if you ask.

How we respond to Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not sell or share personal information and run no advertising trackers. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of any sale or sharing. We do not track visitors across websites, so Do Not Track changes nothing here.

Other parties on this site

  • GitHub Pages stores and serves the site's files, and Cloudflare sits in front of it (delivery, security, cookieless traffic statistics, the paid-link counter). Like any web server, both receive your IP address and browser details to deliver a page and may keep short-lived security logs under their own policies.
  • MailerLite runs our sign-up forms and email. On pages with a sign-up form, MailerLite's scripts load from its servers, so MailerLite receives your IP address and browser details even if you do not subscribe.
  • Impact (impact.com) runs some of our affiliate programs. Its tracking script loads on the home page to verify the site and to attribute clicks on paid links; Impact receives your IP address, browser details and the pages you came from, and may set a cookie. Impact's privacy policy applies to that data.
  • When you click a paid link, you leave this site; the merchant sets its own referral cookie and its policy applies.

These providers may process data in the United States and the European Union. We use them only for the purposes above.

Your rights

You may unsubscribe at any time via the link in any email. You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to its use, by emailing aivguide@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days, honor GDPR and CCPA requests, and do not treat you differently for asking. If you are in the EU or UK you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

Changes are posted here with a new date. Questions? aivguide@gmail.com.
