Studio · Service terms in plain words

How we work together, in plain words.

This is a short summary. The written service agreement you accept is what governs. If this page and your agreement differ, the agreement wins. This page is not legal advice.

01 / the terms

What the agreement covers

Each point below is set out in full in your agreement.

Who you deal with

Your agreement is with Purpose with Presence, the business named on your order form, and with no one else. No contractor, helper or supplier of the studio is a party to it.

How you accept

You accept the terms by ticking an unticked box or signing the order form, before you pay. Nothing starts before then.

Scope and changes

Your plan’s checklist is the scope. Anything outside it is a written change request with its own price and date, agreed before the work starts.

Fair use and volume

Each plan has fair-use limits on locations, accounts and monthly comments, messages and reviews. If you go past them we tell you first, and you choose to stay within the limit or move to the Custom plan.

Approvals

You approve work in a weekly batch. If you do not answer within the window set in your agreement, the items stay unsent unless you have given us a written rule for routine replies. Nothing is published on your behalf without an approval or a rule.

What we need from you

Access by collaborator invite (never your passwords), accurate facts about your business, and answers within the times in the agreement. Delays on your side move our dates.

Accounts and content stay yours

Your domain, profiles, inboxes and ad accounts are in your name. Content you give us stays yours. We are users you can remove at any time, and if you leave you keep everything.

Our portfolio

We show your work in our portfolio only with your written permission, and you can withdraw it.

Platforms set their own rules

Google, Meta and other platforms, web hosts, payment processors and AI tools can change their rules, suspend accounts, remove content or go offline. We do not control them and are not liable for what they do, to the extent the law allows. We tell you promptly when something changes.

Spam and bot protection

Filtering reduces abuse and spam. It cannot remove all of it.

No guarantee of results; the service is provided as is

We do not guarantee rankings, traffic, followers, reviews, bookings or revenue. We promise the work in your plan, done with reasonable skill and care, and a monthly report of what changed. Beyond that, the service is provided as is, and we disclaim other warranties to the extent the law allows.

AI tools and checking

We use software, AI tools included, to draft work, and a person checks every output before it reaches your customers.

Confidentiality and data

We keep your business information confidential and use customer data only to do the work in your plan. We do not sell it. Details are in the agreement and in our privacy page.

Fees, renewal and refunds

Setup is paid half on signing and half at launch. Monthly fees are paid in advance on the 1st. The plan renews month to month after the 3-month minimum.

We do not give refunds. Fees are non-refundable once work for that period has started, setup fees are non-refundable once setup work has begun, and there is no pro-rata refund if you end early. Two exceptions only: if we fail to deliver the agreed work and cannot fix it within the period in your agreement, and any refund right the law does not let you waive. Late payment terms are in the agreement.

Ending the agreement

After the minimum term you can stop by written notice, as set out in the agreement. Either side can end it for serious breach as the agreement states.

Liability

We are not liable for indirect, lost-profit or consequential loss. Our total liability is limited to the fees you paid in the three months before the claim. These limits do not cover our own fraud, gross negligence or wilful misconduct, or anything the law will not let a company limit. Neither side is responsible for events outside its control. You are responsible for the content, instructions and accounts you supply and protect us against claims arising from them.

If we disagree

Write to us first and we will try to resolve it for 30 days. After that, disputes go to final, binding arbitration on an individual basis, not to court, with no class actions and no jury. Small-claims court stays open to both sides. You can opt out of arbitration within 30 days of accepting the terms. Claims must be brought within one year. The governing law and the place for any dispute are named in your agreement.

Suspending or refusing service

We may suspend service if fees are not paid or if the services are used to abuse anyone, and we may decline or end service in the cases the agreement lists.
