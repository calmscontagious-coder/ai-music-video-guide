Guides for local business owners

Straight answers to the questions owners ask us most: what a website really costs, why a business is missing from Google Maps, and exactly what our monthly plans include. Every price carries the date we read it, and every fact links to its source.

Each guide answers one question a restaurant, salon, trades business, gym or bakery owner asks before spending money on a website or on Google. The answer comes first, then the detail. Prices come from the vendor's own page with the date we read them, and anything we couldn't verify is left out.

If customers can't find you, start with the Google Maps checklist: it takes about ten minutes and costs nothing to fix. If you're pricing a new site, read the restaurant website costs guide; the routes apply to most local businesses. If you're weighing our plans, read the line-by-line package guide.
