03 / process

How a month with us runs

Five steps: a free call, research, the build, launch, then a steady monthly cycle. Nothing starts before a signed agreement, and nothing goes live before your yes.

  1. 01

    Intake

    A free call with the director: your goals in your own words. Access comes by collaborator invite, never by sharing passwords.

  2. 02

    Discovery

    Research on your brand, nearest competitors, local calendar, customers and current tools, ending in a ranked written plan and a proposal.

  3. 03

    Build

    Measurement first, so you can see what moves. Then the site, listings, list and emails, each passing our checks before you see it.

  4. 04

    Launch

    A go-live checklist and an ownership check: every account in your name, our access a user you can remove.

  5. 05

    Monthly cycle

    A Monday digest with one change to try, one batched approval message a week, a one-page report and a 30-minute review each month.

What “above and beyond” means

  • Every plan: one surprise improvement a month (a faster page, a better photo crop, a fixed listing, a clearer service description), named in your report, and the 12-month calendar refreshed each year.
  • Growth adds: one new answer-first page a month, a quarterly “what to stop” review and a yearly listing and citation audit.
  • Signature adds: one new offer tested a month, a quarterly strategy review on site, and a yearly competitor re-scan with a written plan for the next year.

What we will not do

  • Hold your card, pay your vendors, or open your accounts in our name.
  • Buy reviews, reward reviews, ask only happy customers, or post as a customer.
  • Text anyone who has not agreed in writing, text outside 11:00 to 16:00 local time, or text about alcohol.
  • Promise bookings, order volume, rankings, review counts or AI citations.
  • Use fake countdowns, fake low-stock lines, or stock photos passed off as your work.
  • Record what visitors type, or run session replays before consent and your lawyer’s yes.
  • Change your prices, services, safety statements or hours before your written yes.

04 / the bar

What every page passes before launch

Your customers see your site on a phone, in a hurry. These are the checks it clears before you are asked to approve it, and again every month.

Speed
Google’s mobile performance score 90 or higher; largest paint in 2.5 s or less, layout shift 0.1 or less, response to taps in 200 ms or less.
Access
Zero serious or critical issues in an automated accessibility scan; WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; touch targets of at least 24 px; visible keyboard focus.
Phones
Readable at 390 px wide with no sideways scroll, checked at 375, 390, 768 and 1440 px.
Facts
Every number, name and date traced to a source; no invented reviews, scarcity or borrowed photos.
Promises
Every form, link, booking and checkout path tried before launch, and consent tested: decline it and no marketing pixel loads.

Plain HTML, CSS and a small script on this page: no framework, cookieless analytics, no ad pixels. See our work on the home page.

05 / questions

Questions owners ask

Short answers on cost, ownership, contracts and results. The written agreement is what counts; these match it.

How much does it cost?

Three monthly plans. Essentials is $950 a month plus $2,500 setup. Growth is $1,750 a month plus $5,200 setup. Signature is $2,450 a month plus $6,500 setup. Each has a 3-month minimum, then runs month to month. One-time projects such as a new site or theme are quoted from the services list.

Your own software is billed by each vendor to your card, never through us. Your proposal lists every tool with its current price, checked on the vendor’s page that day, plus card fees and any ad spend.

How long until things are live?

Measurement goes in first, so there is a baseline before anything changes. Your proposal includes a dated 30, 60 and 90-day plan for your plan. Dates hold when feedback comes back within two business days, and each deliverable includes two rounds of changes.

Who owns the accounts and the data?

You do. Your domain, hosting, store, booking system, analytics, email platform, forms and ad accounts are in your business’s legal name, on your card. We are added as a user you can remove at any time. If you leave, you keep everything, and we hand over the theme, the files and a written list of every automation we set up.

Is there a contract?

Yes. A written service agreement is signed before any work starts: the scope (your plan’s checklist), fees, term, what you provide and by when, ownership, confidentiality and termination. Setup is paid half on signing and half at launch; monthly fees are paid in advance on the 1st. Anything outside your plan is a written change order with a price and a date, agreed before work begins.

How do I cancel?

After the 3-month minimum the plan runs month to month, so you can stop by written notice as set out in the agreement. You keep every account and file. If our prices change, existing clients get 30 days’ written notice.

Who does the work, and who checks it?

A director, a person, runs your account, signs off every piece of work and does the photo days. We use software, AI tools included, to draft and build, and we say so openly.

Before you see anything your customers will see, it passes our design, accessibility, speed and fact checks. Then the director signs it off, and nothing goes live until you say yes. A chat assistant on your site always says it is not a person.

What do you need from me?

A free intake call, in your own words. Then access by collaborator invite (never your passwords), your photos, your service list, menu or product list, prices, hours, and any safety or allergen statements in writing. After launch: one batched approval message a week, answered within two business days where you can, and a 30-minute review once a month.

Do you guarantee results?

No. We do not promise bookings, order numbers, rankings, review counts or mentions in AI answers. We promise the work in your plan, done to the standards on this page, and a monthly report that shows what changed and what we will try next.

Are you local or remote?

Most of the work is remote and runs on a weekly rhythm. Photo days and the Signature quarterly on-site review need the director in person, so distance matters for those: tell us where you are on the call and we will say plainly what we can cover.

Who is this for?

Local businesses with customers nearby: salons and barbers, clinics and practices, trades and home services, gyms and studios, shops, restaurants and cafés, and service professionals. If a plan does not fit your business, we will say so on the call.
