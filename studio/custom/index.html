The three plans cover most local businesses. If you run several locations, many accounts or a very busy audience, Custom gives you the same work at a larger scale, quoted in writing after a free check, so nothing is a surprise.

01 / who it is for

You are a fit if any one of these is true

These are the limits of the three plans, counted as a three-month average. Past any one of them, the work is bigger than a fixed price can cover fairly, so we quote it.

Locations
More than 3 locations, each with its own Google profile, hours and reviews.
Accounts
More than 6 accounts or channels we manage for you, such as social profiles, review sites and message inboxes.
Volume
More than 300 comments, messages and reviews a month across all your accounts.
Hours
A need for more than about 12 director hours a month.

If none of these is true, one of the three plans is the better fit, and we will say so on the call.

02 / what is included

What you get, and what you do not

Custom starts from the Signature plan and adds handling for more places and more messages. Every piece of work is checked by a person before it reaches your customers.

Included

  • Everything in Signature.
  • Comments, messages and reviews on the accounts we manage for you: spam, scams and abuse filtered, replies drafted for your approval.
  • One weekly approval batch for each location, and one monthly report that covers all of them.
  • A written voice and look for each brand you run. Every post and reply is checked against it before it goes out, so a brand sounds the same in March as it did in January.
  • Reports that say what changed, what we saw and what we will try next.

Not included

  • Watching your accounts at night or on weekends, unless the written quote says so.
  • Promises about rankings, traffic, followers, reviews or revenue.
  • Removing every spam or abusive message. Filtering reduces abuse; it cannot remove all of it, and each platform sets its own rules.
  • Buying followers or reviews, or replying as a customer.
  • Legal advice, crisis communications, or anything that needs a lawyer.
  • Ad spend and your own software, which are billed to your card by each vendor.

03 / how the quote works

A free check, a written quote, then a signed agreement

Custom is priced as a monthly base that starts at or above the Signature fee, plus a stated amount for each location, account and block of messages beyond Signature’s limits. You see every number in writing before you sign.

  1. 01

    Free check

    We look at your locations, accounts and the last three months of volume. It costs nothing and carries no obligation.

  2. 02

    Written quote

    You get a quote that lists the base, each add-on and the director’s hours it assumes. If the numbers do not fit, we say so.

  3. 03

    Signed agreement

    Nothing starts before a written service agreement is signed. A plain summary of the terms is here; the signed agreement is what governs.

  4. 04

    A start date

    The studio takes a limited number of custom clients at a time. If it is full, you get a start date, not a rushed job.

  5. 05

    The monthly cycle

    A weekly approval batch, a monthly report and a 30-minute review. Setup is quoted in writing and starts at the Signature setup fee.

04 / fair use

Fair use on the three plans

Each plan covers a fixed amount of managed places and messages, counted as a three-month average. If you go past it, we tell you before anything changes, and you choose: stay within the limit or move to Custom.

Essentials
1 location, 2 accounts, 40 comments, messages and reviews a month.
Growth
2 locations, 4 accounts, 150 comments, messages and reviews a month.
Signature
3 locations, 6 accounts, 300 comments, messages and reviews a month.

05 / approvals

How work is approved each week

You approve in one batched message a week for each location. Replies to complaints, anything that mentions a price, a refund, health, safety or a legal point, and anything we have not sent before always wait for your yes. Routine thank-you replies can go out on a rule you set in writing.

  • Response times we can keep: we answer your emails within one business day, and the weekly batch goes out on the same day each week. We do not offer instant replies or round-the-clock cover.
  • Silence: if a batch gets no answer within the window set in your agreement, the items stay unsent. Nothing is published on your behalf without your approval or a written rule.

06 / what you keep

Every account stays in your name

Your domain, profiles, inboxes, booking and ad accounts are in your business’s legal name, on your card. We are added as a user you can remove at any time. If you leave, you keep everything, and we hand over the files and a written list of what was set up.
