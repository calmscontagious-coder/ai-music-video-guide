How Seedance pricing works

Seedance's Music-to-Video mode charges by the second of your audio: about 2 credits/second at 480p and 4 credits/second at 720p, with 1080p higher. The exact total always shows before you press Generate, and failed renders are refunded automatically. Credits cost roughly $0.04–$0.07 each depending on your plan (from $9.90/month), and there's a free credit to claim every day. This calculator uses ~$0.05/credit as a mid-plan estimate — check the live meter for your exact cost.
