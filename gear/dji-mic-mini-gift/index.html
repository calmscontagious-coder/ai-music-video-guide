01 / verdict

The two-mic set with the case

our verdict

For someone who films on a phone, clean sound is the upgrade reviewers single out, and the Mic Mini is the small, simple way to get it. The two-transmitter set covers interviews and two-person videos, and the case charges everything in a bag. Pick a bigger system only if they need a backup recording on every take or a level display.

04 / questions

Questions before you buy

Does the DJI Mic Mini work with an iPhone?

Yes, through the receiver's plug; reviewers note that Lightning iPhones need a separate adapter and that the receiver sticks out from the phone.

One mic or two?

Two if they ever film with another person or want a spare; the two-mic set also comes with the charging case.

Does it work with cameras?

Yes. Both reviews we read used it with cameras as well as phones, and it pairs directly with some DJI action and pocket cameras.
