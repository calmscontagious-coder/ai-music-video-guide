04 / questions

Questions before you buy

Are the Sony MDR-7506 good for a beginner?

Yes. They are closed-back, so they suit recording as well as listening, and they need no amplifier for a laptop or audio interface.

Can the cable be replaced?

Not easily. The coiled cable is fixed, so a pair with a detachable cable such as the ATH-M50x suits someone hard on cables.

Do the ear pads wear out?

The coating flakes after long use, owners report. Replacement pads are cheap and fit in a few minutes.
