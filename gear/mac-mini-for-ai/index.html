01 / verdict

Buy the M6 with 24GB, not the base model

our verdict

For AI music and AI video made in the cloud, plus a DAW and editing, the M6 chip is enough. Spend on memory, because it cannot be added later, and start at 512GB, because the 256GB drive is reported to read at about half the speed. Pick the M5 Pro only if running large local models or Thunderbolt 5 storage is the point.

05 / questions

Questions before you buy

Is 16GB enough on a Mac mini for AI music and video?

For cloud generators and light editing, yes. For local AI models or large sample libraries, 24GB is the safer start, and memory cannot be added later.

Should I get the M6 or the M5 Pro?

The M6 for most creators. The M5 Pro makes sense when running large local models is the point, because it moves 307GB/s against 170GB/s and takes up to 64GB, or when you need Thunderbolt 5.

Should I buy the 256GB Mac mini?

Avoid it if you can. Reported benchmarks put its SSD at about half the read speed of the 512GB model, and the storage is soldered, so it cannot be upgraded.

How much memory do local AI models need?

Roughly the parameter count times half a byte at 4-bit, plus 3 to 4GB for macOS and room for context. That puts 30B-class models on 24GB to 32GB and 70B-class models on 48GB to 64GB.
