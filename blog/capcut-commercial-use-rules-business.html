Can You Use CapCut for Business? The Commercial Use Rules in Plain English

Quick answer: Yes, within limits. CapCut's licence sorts its library into items marked for commercial use, which may go in ads and promotions, and everything else, which is for personal, non-commercial videos only. One unmarked item makes the whole video non-commercial. Its general music library is personal-use only.

Can a business post videos made in CapCut?

Yes. The limits in CapCut's Materials License Agreement attach to CapCut's own library, which it calls Platform Materials: video clips, pictures, stickers, text templates, fonts, sound effects, effects, filters and animations. They also attach to its music libraries.

So the question for any business video is simple: which library items are in this edit, and how is each one marked?

The agreement is written for people aged 13 years and over, and anyone under 18, or under the age of majority where they live, needs a parent or guardian's consent. It also says CapCut may update the terms, with the date of the last update shown at the top, so check it again before a large campaign.

Which templates, effects and fonts can go in an ad?

Only the ones marked for it. The agreement defines two kinds of material:

KindHow you spot itWhat you may do
Dual Use MaterialsCarry a mark such as "commercial use" or "commercial"Personal use, plus the limited commercial uses listed below
Non-commercial Use MaterialsNo commercial markPersonal, non-commercial videos only

For a video that contains only commercial-marked materials, the agreement allows:

  • Online promotion, marketing and advertising for goods or services, including on e-commerce platforms, as long as the video is not used to vouch for the authenticity, legality or quality of the product.
  • Promotion and advertising through other online media.
  • Printed brochures, posters and similar items that are not for resale.
  • Newspaper, magazine and book pages, excluding front and back covers.
  • Outdoor advertising such as banners, display windows and billboards.

Then comes the rule that catches people. If a video mixes commercial-marked material with even 1 unmarked item, the agreement says the whole video may be used for personal, non-commercial purposes only.

CapCut keeps a collection of templates for commercial use, which is the place to start. Its help centre also says that Pro includes commercial-use assets, so a business that leans on the library should look at CapCut Pro. Pro also lists 1 TB of storage, and new subscribers in supported regions get a 7-day trial. A paid plan widens what you can use; it does not replace the mark on each item, which still decides whether that item may appear in an ad.

Can I use CapCut's music in a business video?

Not the general library. The agreement describes two music libraries:

  • Sounds are for individual users whose video is for personal entertainment and non-commercial purposes. The agreement says you should not use a Sound to promote a brand or business unless you have obtained the rights yourself.
  • Commercial Sounds are for brands, businesses and advertisers. A video using one may be shown within 3 places: CapCut, TikTok and TikTok for Business. Anywhere else needs a separate licence from the rights holder.

The clean route for most businesses is music you already hold the rights to: a track you commissioned, licensed elsewhere with the paperwork, or wrote yourself. If you add music that did not come from CapCut's licensed libraries, the agreement has you confirm that you created it or hold the permissions.

What is off limits even with commercial-marked items?

The agreement lists uses that are never allowed for its materials. The ones a small business is most likely to meet:

  • Advertising for tobacco, pharmaceutical, healthcare, herbal or medical products, including dietary supplements.
  • Advertising for adult entertainment venues, escort or dating services.
  • Using a library item as your logo, trademark, brand name or part of one.
  • Selling or handing out a library item on its own, outside a finished video.
  • Editing the library material outside CapCut after export.
  • Removing copyright or trademark notices from the materials.

A clinic, pharmacy or supplement shop should read that first line twice. For those businesses the safest edit uses only footage, type and music the business owns.

Does the United States have different terms?

Yes. The agreement states that people using CapCut in the United States are governed by a separate CapCut US Materials License Agreement. We could not open that document during our research, so everything above describes the general agreement. If you are in the US, read the US version inside the app or on CapCut's site before running paid ads.

How do I check a video before it runs as an ad?

  1. List every library item in the edit: template, stickers, fonts, effects, stock clips, music.
  2. Confirm each one carries a commercial mark. Replace any that do not, or use your own asset.
  3. Check the music separately. Use your own licensed track, or a Commercial Sound if the ad will run only on the 3 permitted platforms.
  4. Check your industry against the prohibited list.
  5. Save a note of what you used and the date, in case the question comes up later.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use CapCut for commercial purposes?

Yes, with limits set by CapCut's Materials License Agreement. Library items marked for commercial use may appear in online promotion, advertising and certain print and outdoor uses. Unmarked items are for personal, non-commercial videos only, and one unmarked item makes the whole video non-commercial.

Can I use CapCut templates in ads for my business?

Only templates and assets that carry a commercial mark. CapCut's agreement calls these Dual Use Materials and allows them in online marketing and advertising. CapCut keeps a collection of templates for commercial use. Check every element inside the template, because a single unmarked sticker or font changes what the whole video may be used for.

Is CapCut music copyright free for business use?

No. The general Sounds library is licensed for personal, non-commercial videos. Commercial Sounds are available to businesses, but a video using one may be shown only within 3 places: CapCut, TikTok and TikTok for Business. For other platforms, use music you hold the rights to.

Which businesses cannot use CapCut library materials in ads?

The agreement bars its materials from advertising for tobacco, pharmaceutical, healthcare, herbal or medical products, including dietary supplements, and for adult entertainment venues, escort and dating services. Businesses in those fields can still edit their own footage, but should keep CapCut's library items out of promotional videos.

Do the commercial use rules differ in the United States?

Yes. CapCut's general agreement says users in the United States are governed by a separate CapCut US Materials License Agreement. We could not open that version during our research, so this guide describes the general agreement only. US businesses should read the US document before running paid ads.
