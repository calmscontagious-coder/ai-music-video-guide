CapCut Templates: How to Use Them, and Why Some Do Not Show Up

How do I use a CapCut template?

A template is a finished edit with empty seats: the cuts, timing, text styles and effects are done, and you supply the footage. The working order is short; steps 2 and 5 are CapCut's own instructions, and the rest is ordinary editing.

  1. Open the Templates tab and pick one that fits the length and shape of your footage.
  2. Replace the template's clips with your own photos or video.
  3. Rewrite the placeholder text.
  4. Open the result in the editor if you want to change more: swap an effect, fix a cut, change the audio.
  5. Go to the end of the timeline and delete the branded ending clip.
  6. Export.

CapCut's plan guide confirms that Pro templates work on desktop, phone and web, and that you replace the template's clips with your own before editing further.

Why can't I find a template someone else used?

Because the library is not the same for everyone. CapCut's help page on missing templates says this is by design, and gives the reasons for each version:

VersionWhat you seeWhy
Phone appThe widest and newest libraryNew templates and effects are released here first
Desktop appA curated subsetMany arrive weeks or months later, or not at all
Web editorThe smallest, general setNo personalised feed; heavy effects are left out for performance

Even on the phone, CapCut gives five reasons a template may not appear. Three you can act on:

  • Region. Some templates are released only in certain countries because of licensing or local campaigns.
  • App version. An older version may not show newer templates. Update first.
  • Device. Some effects need hardware an older phone does not have.

The other two are gradual rollouts, where a new template reaches only some accounts at first, and account eligibility, such as age requirements.

CapCut's suggested workaround for desktop users: apply the template on your phone, let the project sync through CapCut's cloud, then open it on the computer to finish. Note that cloud space is a subscriber benefit.

Are CapCut templates free?

Some are. The pricing page lists free templates on the Free plan and all premium templates on Pro. A premium template can often be previewed on a free account, but the export screen will ask for a subscription. CapCut's help centre describes this as intended: you may try paid items, and the check happens at export.

If you use premium templates every week, the subscription is the tidy answer. Pro lists all premium templates, 1,200 monthly AI credits and 1 TB of storage. You can see CapCut Pro, and new subscribers in supported regions get a 7-day trial.

How do I remove the watermark from a template video?

Delete the ending clip. CapCut's template watermark page says the mark normally comes from a default ending clip or a built-in template element, not from the export.

  1. Open the project after applying the template.
  2. Go to the end of the timeline.
  3. Select the CapCut-branded ending clip and delete it.
  4. Check the other tracks for an overlay layer with a logo.
  5. Preview, then export.

If a mark remains, CapCut says to contact its support; another of its help pages says premium templates and effects add a watermark on the free version. Our guide to exporting without a watermark covers that case.

Why does my template export look soft?

Check the project size. CapCut's help page on blurry video notes that starting from a template in the web editor may lock the project at 1080p, and that the phone app sets the timeline to the resolution of the first clip you import. Put your sharpest clip in first, and export at the resolution of your footage. The numbers for each platform are in our export settings guide.

Can I use a template for my business?

Only if everything in it is marked for commercial use. CapCut's Materials License Agreement allows promotional use for items carrying a commercial mark, and says that mixing in even 1 unmarked item makes the whole video personal-use only. A template is a bundle of items: clips, fonts, stickers, effects, sometimes music.

Start from CapCut's templates for commercial use, replace the music with audio you hold the rights to, and read our plain-English guide to CapCut commercial use rules before running anything as an ad.

When is a template the wrong tool?

When the footage has its own rhythm. A template forces your clips into someone else's timing, which is fine for a quick product reveal and awkward for a song with its own structure. Musicians usually get a better result cutting to their own track; see beat-sync editing for that method.

It is also the wrong tool when you need a look nobody else has. A popular template is, by definition, already on other accounts. Use it for speed, and build from a blank project when the video has to be yours.

How do I use a template in CapCut?

Open the Templates tab, choose a template, and replace its clips with your own photos or video. Edit the placeholder text, then open the project in the editor for further changes. Before exporting, go to the end of the timeline and delete the branded ending clip so the video saves without it.

Why is a CapCut template not showing on my device?

CapCut says template availability depends on the version you use, your region, your app version and your device. New templates are released on the phone app first; desktop shows a curated subset and the web editor a smaller set. Update the app, and look for the template on your phone.

Are CapCut templates free to use?

The Free plan includes free templates, and Pro includes all premium templates. You can preview a premium template without paying, but CapCut checks the project at export and asks for a subscription if premium items are present. New subscribers in supported regions can try Pro for 7 days.
