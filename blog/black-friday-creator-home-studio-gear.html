How to tell a real sale from a holiday label

  • Look at the price history, not the crossed-out price. A free price tracker shows whether today's price is actually lower than last month's.
  • Check the exact model. Memory, storage, color and bundle all change the listing; the cards name the configuration we mean.
  • Check the seller. Memory cards in particular are often faked; buy from the brand or the store itself.
  • Check the return window and the delivery date before you order a gift.
