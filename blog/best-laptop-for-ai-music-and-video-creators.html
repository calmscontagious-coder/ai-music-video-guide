Short answer: if your AI music and video tools run in the browser, buy a MacBook Air M5 with 24GB of memory. If you export long timelines or run local models, buy a MacBook Pro 14 with the M5 Pro and 48GB. Pick an NVIDIA laptop such as the ROG Zephyrus G14 only if your local image or video workflow is built around CUDA.

Three questions that decide the laptop

  • Where does the AI run? Cloud generators for music and video do the heavy work on their servers, so a light laptop handles them. Local models are the reason to buy more memory and cooling.
  • How much memory for local models? A model's weights need roughly its parameter count times the bytes per weight, about 0.5 bytes at 4-bit, and macOS keeps 3 to 4GB for itself (llama.cpp sizing guide). By that math, 24GB fits about a 30B model at 4-bit with a short context, 48GB a 70B model at 4-bit tightly, and 64GB or more comfortably. Memory is soldered on every laptop here, so choose once.
  • Mac or NVIDIA? ComfyUI supports Apple silicon, but its Windows portable build is made for NVIDIA cards (ComfyUI). On a Windows laptop, the GPU's video memory, not system memory, sets the model size limit.

For music production, Production Expert's 2026 guide puts MIDI-heavy sessions at 16GB to 24GB, mixing with sample libraries at 32GB, and large orchestral templates at 64GB (Production Expert).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a powerful laptop to make AI music?+

No, if you use cloud generators. They run on the provider's servers, so a MacBook Air handles them. You need more memory and cooling only for a large DAW session or local models.

How much RAM do I need for local AI on a laptop?+

About 24GB for a 30B model at 4-bit with a short context, 48GB for a 70B model at 4-bit, and 64GB or more for comfort. Laptop memory is soldered, so buy what you will need.

Mac or Windows for local Stable Diffusion and ComfyUI?+

Both work. ComfyUI supports Apple silicon, but its Windows portable build targets NVIDIA cards, and many local image and video workflows assume CUDA. On Windows, the GPU's video memory limits model size.

Is the fanless MacBook Air fine for video editing?+

For short edits, yes. Reviewers report it slows down under long heavy loads, so a MacBook Pro suits long 4K exports better.
