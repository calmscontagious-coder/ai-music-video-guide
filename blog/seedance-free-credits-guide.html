Seedance Free Credits in 2026: Every Verified Way to Try It at $0

If you want to test Seedance without paying, there is exactly one official path, and it is smaller than the copycat sites claim. Seedance free credits come as a single small daily allowance — sign in, and you can "Claim your free credit every 24 hours" (Source: seeddance.video). That is the honest answer: the signup link is the free credit. Everything else you have read about "100 daily credits, no watermark, no card" is coming from imitator domains, not the real product.

We dug into the actual site to map what that free credit gets you, how the per-second credit math works, and where the free tier hits its wall. Here is the straight version.

What do the daily Seedance free credits actually get you?

The official free tier is a trial sample, not a starter pack. Signed-in users get one free credit refreshed every 24 hours (Source: seeddance.video). It lets you sign in, look around the generators and read the credit counter before you pay. On its own it does not cover a single render.

Two limits matter before you build anything on it:

  • It is not watermark-free. Watermark-free MP4 and PNG exports are listed as a paid-plan feature across Basic, Pro, Max, and Pro Max (Source: seeddance.video/pricing). Anything you make on the free credit carries the mark.
  • It does not include a commercial license. The commercial license "covering every output" is bundled only with the paid subscription tiers (Source: seeddance.video/pricing). Free-tier output is for testing, not client work or paid media.

So the free credit answers one question — "do I like how these engines render?" — and nothing beyond it. That is still useful, because Seedance bundles a lot of engines behind that one login.

How does the per-second credit math work?

Credits are billed per second of video, at a rate set by the model and the resolution. Seedance 2.5 costs 12 credits a second at 480p, 23 at 720p and 48 at 1080p, for clips of 4 to 30 seconds (Source: seeddance.video Seedance 2.5 page, read October 25, 2026). Seedance 2.0 costs 20 credits a second at 480p and 40 at 720p, for clips of 4 to 15 seconds (Source: Seedance 2.0 page, read October 25, 2026), though its generator showed 40 credits at the default 5 seconds when we checked, which does not match that rate. The charge for your chosen length and resolution appears on screen before you commit, so read the counter.

Now apply that to a single daily free credit. One credit buys a twelfth of a second of Seedance 2.5 at 480p, and the generator will not render less than 4 seconds. Here is the reality in a table:

Walk through a concrete example. A 5-second Seedance 2.5 clip at 720p costs 5 × 23 = 115 credits, and at 480p it costs 5 × 12 = 60. At one free credit a day, the 480p clip takes 60 days of claims, and that assumes you claim every day and the credits stay in your balance, which we have not tested. Seeddance’s separate Music to Video tool bills less, 2 credits a second at 480p and 4 at 720p, but it takes a whole song, the site does not name the model behind it, and its 5-second minimum still costs 5 × 2 = 10 credits (Source: seeddance.video Music to Video page, read October 25, 2026). The free credit is a look around the product, not a production budget.

The takeaway: treat the free daily credit as a "kick the tires" sample. To make anything you would actually post, you need a paid credit balance. If you want to model the math for your own clip lengths, our credit calculator lets you plug in seconds and resolution.

Is there any other free path?

Not a documented one. Beyond the recurring 24-hour credit, we could not verify a separate standalone free trial on the official site. Promotional credit events may happen from time to time, but they are not published as a persistent offer, so we would not plan around one. If you see a page promising a big free bundle, check the domain first.

When legitimate promotions do run, they come from the official seeddance.video domain, are time-boxed, and never ask for a credit card to "unlock" free credits. A genuine referral or launch promo shows up inside your signed-in account dashboard and any bonus credits land directly in your account balance — not on a third-party landing page. Fake offers almost always live on look-alike domains, promise permanent free access, and push you to enter payment details or install something before you see anything. Those are all signals to close the tab. When in doubt, navigate to the official site directly rather than following a link, and confirm any credit bump actually appears in your balance before you trust it.

Watch out for the copycat "free credit" sites

Search "seedance free credits" and you will hit a wall of aggregator and clone domains — names like seedance.tv, seedance.ai, and seeddance.io — advertising "100 daily credits, no watermark, no credit card." Those numbers do not match the official seeddance.video, whose free tier is a single small daily credit with no watermark removal and no commercial rights (Source: comparison of seeddance.video vs seedance.tv / seeddance.io). Overstated free tiers are the tell. If a site guarantees free forever, watermark-free, or commercial rights on a free plan, it is not the product we tested.

What do you get if you upgrade?

The free credit exists to funnel you toward the paid plans, and the paid plans are where watermark-free exports, commercial rights, higher resolutions, and a real credit balance live (Source: seeddance.video/pricing). Paid plans start at $9.90/month for Basic on annual billing, or $19.90 billed monthly for 200 credits (Source: seeddance.video/pricing). Here is the full table:

What the one subscription actually covers is the strong part. A single Seedance credit pool bundles 30+ engines, including Seedance 2, Sora 2, Sora 2 Pro, and Veo 3 for video, image models like Flux Kontext, Flux Krea, SeeDream 4 and Nano Banana, plus Suno for music (Source: seeddance.video). OpenAI discontinued the Sora app on April 26, 2026 and lists September 24, 2026 as the Sora API shutdown date (Source: OpenAI Help Center, read October 25, 2026), so we cannot verify what renders under the Sora labels now. If you were otherwise paying for several video models separately, that consolidation is the real pitch. For a head-to-head on the video engines, see our Sora 2 vs Veo 3 vs Seedance comparison.

The feature worth testing first: audio sync

If you are a musician, test this first. Seedance can beat-sync visuals to an uploaded audio track — you upload an audio sample to lock tempo and mood, and outputs arrive with a two-channel mix (dialogue tracked to mouth shapes, room tone, Foley, and score) (Source: seeddance.video). It is the capability that separates it from generic text-to-video tools, and one short paid clip is enough to see whether the sync holds up on your track. Honest bottom line: the free daily credit is a genuine, no-cost way to look around the generators, but it is a sample, not a production tool. It carries a watermark, grants no commercial rights, and does not cover a single render on its own. Use it to learn the interface, then judge the output on one short paid clip. The signup link is the whole free tier — that is where to start.

Does the Seedance free tier put a watermark on your videos?+
