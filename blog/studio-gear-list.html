Frequently asked questions

What should I buy first for making AI music at home?+

Closed-back studio headphones. They reveal bass and harsh highs that laptop speakers hide, so you can judge which generated takes are worth keeping.

Do I need an expensive microphone for an AI voice clone?+

No. A quiet room and steady delivery matter more. A dynamic mic in a room softened with blankets usually beats a costly condenser in an echoey room.
