Seedance Promo Codes in 2026: What Actually Works (We Tested Them)

If you searched for a Seedance promo code, you probably hit three coupon sites claiming three different discounts, none of which worked at checkout. We went looking too, tested what the aggregators listed, and read the actual pricing page. Here is the honest version: there is no verified working coupon code that lowers the price at seeddance.video right now. What does lower the cost is real, official, and boring, and it is the same lever on every plan.

This is not a "here's a secret code" article, because those articles are usually wrong and the code fails at the last screen. It's the version that saves you the twenty minutes of pasting dead codes and tells you where the genuine savings are.

The honest truth about the Seedance promo code you are hunting for

We could not confirm a single working coupon code against the seeddance.video checkout. The codes floating around aggregator pages — strings like TYGSNTTST, YOTNEG, and VE15 — are not published or confirmed by any official Seedance source, so treat them as unverified and almost certainly dead (Source: Dealspotr). Random alphanumeric codes with no traceable origin are the tell-tale mark of auto-scraped or fabricated coupon listings, not real promos.

The tell is in the numbers themselves. Look at what the "Seedance" coupon aggregators claim, side by side:

Most of those codes are for the wrong Seedance anyway

Here is the part almost no coupon page mentions: "Seedance" is a contested name. A cluster of near-identical clone and SEO domains competes for it — seedance.tv, seeddance.io, seedance.ai, seedance2.ai, seedance2.studio, seedomni.ai, aiseedance.pro — each publishing its own pricing and its own free-tier claims. Most aggregator coupon pages point at "seedance.tv," a different look-alike domain from the seeddance.video product (Source: Dealspotr). So a code "for Seedance" often does not even target the checkout you are trying to pay at.

Some of those clones also overstate the offer in exactly the way you should distrust. One advertises a "Free Plan, No Credit Card Required" with "100 daily credits, no watermark." The source-of-truth product at seeddance.video makes no such promise — and a free, no-watermark, no-card tier is precisely the guarantee that should make you suspicious, because watermark-free output is a paid feature everywhere it is real. The real discount: annual billing, about 30% off

The genuine cost lever is not a code. It is annual billing. Seedance's paid plans run roughly $9.90 to $59.90 per month, and switching to annual billing takes about 30% off the monthly-equivalent rate (Source: seeddance.video). That is the official promo. It applies to every tier, it survives checkout, and it does not depend on a code someone scraped.

The four paid tiers are named Basic, Pro, Max, and Pro Max. The site also runs an urgency banner — "Price increase coming soon! Subscribe now to lock in low prices!" — which means the only time-sensitive "promo" is committing to annual billing to lock the current rate before it moves. Ignore the countdown; urgency banners are a sales tactic. But the annual discount underneath it is real, and it beats any coupon code we could find. Verify the current numbers on the Seedance pricing page before you commit, since plans do change.

The bigger lever nobody markets: the credit math

Even the annual discount is smaller than the savings you control at generation time. Seedance runs on credits, and credits are priced per second of generated video, scaled by resolution — roughly 2 credits per second at 480p, about 4 per second at 720p, and more at 1080p and 4K (Source: campaign ground-truth, corroborated by clone-site pricing pages). The estimated credit cost is shown before you commit each render, and failed renders refund the credits.

That per-second, per-resolution pricing is the real discount lever: your spend is a choice, not a fixed fee. A ten-second 480p draft costs a fraction of a ten-second 4K final. In our runs, the discipline that saves the most credits is simple: draft short and low, then only spend the big credits on the version you have already decided you like. No coupon does that for you. The credit calculator will tell you what a given clip costs at each resolution before you burn anything.

Seedance is one subscription over 30-plus engines — Seedance 2 and 2.5, Sora 2 and Sora 2 Pro, Veo 3, Flux Kontext, Flux Krea, SeeDream 4, Nano Banana, and Suno for music — all drawing on the same credit pool (Source: seeddance.video). That matters for cost planning: the "discount" of paying once for the whole stack instead of subscribing to each model separately is larger than any coupon, and it is why matching the cheap engine to the job saves more than any code. For musicians weighing which engine to spend credits on, our engine guide for musicians covers the trade-offs.

Use the free daily credit to test before you pay

You do not need a code to try Seedance. The site advertises a small daily sample — "+1 Credit, claim your free credit every 24 hours" — plus a first render "on us" (Source: seeddance.video). That is enough to see whether the output quality justifies a subscription. Be clear about the limits, though: the free daily credit is not watermark-free and does not include a commercial license. Watermark-free MP4 and PNG exports, and a commercial license covering every output, come with the paid Basic, Pro, Max, and Pro Max tiers only. So the free path is for testing, not for producing anything you plan to publish or sell.

"Free Seedance" methods are a different product

Search results are also full of "get Seedance free" guides pointing at Rita AI, Dreamina, CapCut, Runway, or the BytePlus ModelArk API. Those run the underlying ByteDance Seedance model on other platforms — they are not the seeddance.video subscription, and the free ones are watermarked, resolution-limited, or require a China phone number (Source: GamsGo). Separately, account-sharing resellers push codes like "SAVE60" that actually deliver about 1% off and route you into a subscription-sharing scheme, not an official discount (Source: GamsGo). If a "code" sends you to a third-party reseller instead of the Seedance checkout, that is the scheme, not a saving.

The bottom line on Seedance discounts

No verified coupon code reduces the price at seeddance.video. The three levers that genuinely lower your cost are all things you control: switch to annual billing for roughly 30% off, use the free daily credit to test before you subscribe, and match resolution and clip length to the job so the per-second credit cost stays low. Stop pasting dead codes. The real discount was never a code — it was the annual toggle and the render settings. Once you have decided Seedance fits your workflow, you can start at the official pricing page, confirm the current annual rate, and skip the coupon hunt entirely.
