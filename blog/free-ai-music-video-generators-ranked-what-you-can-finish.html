Quick answer: At $0 with no watermark, only an open-weights model like Wan 2.2 or LTX-2.3 on your GPU finishes a music video you can post. Every hosted free tier we checked on October 21, 2026 stamps a visible mark, gives no usable credits, or has a mark we couldn't confirm. Clean export starts at $5 to $10 a month.

Sora is gone. OpenAI closed the app on April 26, 2026 and the API on September 24, 2026. In the same months, several other free lanes got smaller.

So we asked a narrower question than which free AI music video generator is best: what can a musician finish at $0? A finished clip is one you'd post under your song. No visible mark, downloadable, long enough to matter. By that test most free tiers finish nothing.

We opened every pricing and policy page below on October 21, 2026. Where a number comes from a third-party tracker rather than the vendor, we say so.

How do we rank what you can finish at $0?

We sort every free lane into one of three outcomes: a clean clip you can post, a watermarked draft you can only use for testing, or nothing without a card. Then we ask how long each clip is and how many you get a day.

A Spotify Canvas loop runs 3 to 8 seconds. A TikTok or Reels teaser wants 8 to 15 seconds. A full video for a three-minute song needs 20 to 40 clips plus an edit. A tier that hands you two 8-second clips a day can finish a Canvas in one sitting and a teaser in two. It can't finish a full video this month.

We didn't score picture quality here. Our no-watermark export test covers the watermark and licensing fine print in more depth. This page is only about the gate between generated and finished.

Which free AI video generators export with no watermark right now?

Only open-weights models you run yourself export clean with no cap. Pika's free plan is also clean, but it ships with 0 monthly credits, so it renders nothing at $0.

Two more lanes might be clean, and we couldn't confirm either. A September 2026 tracker saw no visible mark on PixVerse's free output, and fal.ai's free Grok Imagine sandbox gives five short image-to-video clips a day that we haven't checked. Everything else stamps the download or gives too few credits to render at all.

What's the ranking, lane by lane?

Open weights on your own GPU rank first, because it's the only lane that finishes clean. Below it, we rank by what each lane finishes, then by how many clips a day you get.

Treat each lane as a free AI music video generator on trial, and judge it on the export, not the demo reel. One lane finishes clean. Six give you drafts: five with a visible mark, plus PixVerse, whose mark we couldn't confirm. Four finish nothing you could post at $0, including Grok, whose only free route is a third-party test sandbox.

1. Wan 2.2 or LTX-2.3 on your own GPU

This is the only free lane with no mark and no daily cap. Thunder Compute's open-weights guide, updated October 1, 2026, puts Wan 2.2 at 8 GB of VRAM for the 5B build at 720p under an Apache 2.0 license, which allows commercial use. LTX-2.3 generates picture and sound in one pass and wants 16 GB on a Blackwell card or 32 GB in FP8. The cost is time. Thunder Compute clocks a 5-second 480p clip from the Wan 2.2 5B build at 4 to 6 minutes on an RTX 4090. The 8 GB build leans on memory offloading, so expect smaller cards to run slower. A full video is 30 of those clips plus an edit. If you own the hardware, nothing else on this page competes.

2. Google Flow free tier

Google's Flow credits page says every account gets 50 Flow credits a day with or without a subscription. At 20 credits per Veo 3.1 Fast clip and 10 per Lite clip, that's two Fast or five Lite 8-second clips. Gemini Omni Flash costs 12 credits per 8-second 720p clip, so 50 credits cover four, but one request can create two billed generations. Two catches. Non-subscribers can be blocked from video during peak hours, roughly 2 to 5 PM UTC. And Google's help text reserves the clean export for the Ultra tier, a point a Google product expert restated in a Gemini community thread on Ultra watermarks, so free clips carry the small veo mark. Good enough to storyboard a Canvas. Not a finished one.

3. Dreamina, where Seedance 2.0 and 2.5 live

ByteDance's Seedance models run on Dreamina, and Dreamina's own credits FAQ dated September 10, 2026 gives free users 120 credits a day. The same site's June 26, 2026 guide said 225. Those credits buy Seedance 2.0 Mini or Fast drafts at 720p. The paid plans list "remove the Dreamina watermark in downloads" as a feature, which tells you what the free download carries. This is the most generous marked lane for testing Seedance prompts. It isn't a way to ship.

4. Kling

Kling doesn't publish a fixed free allowance. Its credits policy says daily login credits are issued "during the event" and reset at midnight, and its own FAQ says free-tier exports generally include a watermark, with removal a membership benefit. An independent tracker checked the live figure at 66 credits a day in August 2026, roughly two 5-second clips at 720p, down from an earlier 166. Kling sells credits at $1 for 66, so the free day is worth about a dollar.

5. PixVerse

The same September 2026 tracker logged about 50 credits at signup on PixVerse, then 30 to 60 a day, enough for one or two clips. It saw no visible watermark on free output. PixVerse's pricing page didn't render for us on October 21, 2026, so we can't confirm the mark from the vendor. Make one clip and look at the corner before you plan around it.

6. Hailuo

Hailuo's subscription terms are plain: free users "download videos that include a watermark", and watermark-free downloads start with the Standard plan. The free allowance is a signup offer, logged at 150 credits by the September 2026 tracker, not a daily refill. Hailuo's own paid-versus-free guide, as read on October 21, 2026, lists Standard at $9.99 a month. Its subscription terms, last updated July 14, 2025, list Standard at $14.99 with 1,000 credits a month. Check the price at checkout.

7. Runway

Runway's pricing page gives new accounts 125 one-time credits that never refresh. Gen-4.5 costs 60 credits per 5 seconds, so that's two clips, once. "No watermarks" appears as a Standard-plan feature. Runway now hosts Seedance 2.0 inside the same credit pool, at 116 credits per 4 seconds for Fast and 160 for Pro at 1080p, so a Runway subscriber can test Seedance without a second account.

8. Pika

Pika's free plan is the odd one: no watermark, all models, and 0 credits a month. You generate by buying a credit pack, so the free plan is a storefront with a clean export policy. Starter is $10 a month for 900 credits, still without a commercial license. Clean, yes. Free, no.

9. Luma Dream Machine

Luma's pricing page lists paid plans only, and the trackers describe trial credits rather than a recurring free tier. Plus is $30 a month for 10,000 credits with commercial use, Pro $90 for 40,000 and Ultra $300 for 150,000 (October 21, 2026). Nothing here finishes at $0.

10. Grok Imagine

Free Grok Imagine ended on March 19, 2026, when image and video generation moved behind the $30 SuperGrok plan. Paid accounts draw from a shared weekly pool that video drains faster than chat. The workaround is fal.ai, which gives signed-in users five Grok Imagine Video 1.5 Lite image-to-video clips every rolling 24 hours, with native audio. We haven't checked those exports for a mark. Keep it for tests.

How did free credit allowances change in 2026?

Several vendors cut their free allowances in the same months Sora wound down, and none of them said why. The changes showed up on the vendors' own pages, not in announcements.

  • Dreamina: 225 free credits a day in its June 26, 2026 guide, 120 in its September 10, 2026 FAQ.
  • Grok Imagine: free generation closed March 19, 2026, five weeks before the Sora app shut.
  • Kling: 66 daily credits in an August 2026 tracker check, against an earlier, undated 166.

Treat free tiers as trials, and check any "unlimited free, no watermark" headline against the vendor's own page. Our Sora shutdown guide has migration notes for anyone still holding Sora clips.

What should a musician make at $0 this week?

Use the marked lanes to decide, not to publish. No hosted free AI music video generator on this list gave us a confirmed clean export, so the free week is for choosing an engine.

Run the same 8-second prompt through Dreamina, Flow and Kling on their daily credits, and pick the engine whose motion fits the song. Then spend one month on that engine's cheapest clean tier during release week. On the Seedance site that means the higher month-to-month price, since the $9.90 rate needs a year upfront. If you own a 12 GB or bigger GPU, skip the subscriptions and start with Wan 2.2.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free AI video generator with no watermark and no daily cap?+

Only if you run the model yourself. Wan 2.2 and LTX-2.3 are open weights with no mark and no cap, and Wan 2.2's Apache 2.0 license allows commercial use. Every hosted free tier we checked on October 21, 2026 watermarks the download, gives zero monthly credits like Pika, or has a mark we couldn't confirm from the vendor, like PixVerse.

How many free Seedance videos can I make a day?+

On Dreamina, ByteDance's home for Seedance, free users get 120 credits a day (September 10, 2026), enough for a short watermarked Seedance 2.0 Mini or Fast draft. On the multi-engine site we link, one daily credit is far below the 60 to 100 credits a 5-second 480p Seedance clip costs, so it renders no Seedance video alone. Neither free lane includes a commercial license.

Does Google Flow watermark free Veo videos?+

Yes, for most accounts. The visible-watermark exemption is reserved for the Ultra tier, and Google's documentation now says even Ultra clips carry a visible mark where local rules require one. All Veo output also carries the invisible SynthID fingerprint. The free 50 daily credits buy two Veo 3.1 Fast clips or five Lite clips, each 8 seconds long.

Can I just remove the watermark with a tool?+

You can, and you shouldn't. Clean export is the paid feature on Kling, Hailuo, Dreamina and Runway, so stripping the mark cuts against the terms you accepted, and it doesn't touch invisible fingerprints like SynthID. One month on the cheapest clean tier, $5 to $10 at today's prices, costs less than a takedown or a strike on your channel.

Which free tier is best for a Spotify Canvas?+

A Canvas loops for 3 to 8 seconds, so one clean clip is all you need. Google Flow's 50 daily credits produce two 8-second Veo 3.1 Fast clips, but they carry the veo mark. For a clean loop at $0, render it with Wan 2.2. Otherwise one month of Kling Standard, whose 660 credits make about 20 five-second 720p clips at the tracker's rate, covers an album's worth.

Why did free AI video credits shrink in 2026?+

No vendor has said why. The cuts clustered in the months Sora wound down: Grok closed free generation on March 19, before the Sora app shut on April 26, and Dreamina's own pages dropped from 225 to 120 daily credits between June and September 2026. A tracker logged Kling at 66 credits a day in August, against an earlier, undated 166. Treat free tiers as trials.
