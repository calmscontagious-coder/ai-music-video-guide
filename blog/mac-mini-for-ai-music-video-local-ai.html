Short answer: for AI music, AI video and light local AI, buy the Mac mini M6 with 24GB of memory and 512GB of storage. Go to 32GB if you load large sample libraries or want 30B-class local models. Choose the M5 Pro with 48GB or 64GB only if running large local models is the point. Skip the 256GB base model.

What Apple sells now

The current line has two chips (Apple tech specs):

  • M6: 12-core CPU and 12-core GPU, 16GB to 32GB of memory, 256GB to 2TB of storage, three Thunderbolt 4 ports. Memory bandwidth is 153GB/s on the 16GB model and 170GB/s on 24GB and 32GB.
  • M5 Pro: 15-core or 18-core CPU, 16-core or 20-core GPU, 24GB to 64GB of memory, 512GB to 8TB of storage, three Thunderbolt 5 ports and 307GB/s of memory bandwidth.
  • Both: HDMI, 2.5Gb Ethernet (10Gb as an option), two 10Gb/s USB-C ports and a headphone jack on the front, Wi-Fi 7, and a 5 by 5 inch body.

Memory and storage are fixed when you buy. Digital Trends reports that the SSD on the new models is soldered to the board, unlike the M4 model's removable module (Digital Trends).

How much memory you need

For local AI models. A model's weights need roughly its parameter count times the bytes per weight: about 0.5 bytes at 4-bit and 1 byte at 8-bit. macOS keeps about 3 to 4GB for itself (llama.cpp sizing guide). That rule counts weights only; the context window needs extra room on top. By that math:

  • 16GB leaves about 12GB: 7B to 13B models at 4-bit.
  • 24GB leaves about 20GB: a 14B model at 8-bit, or around 30B at 4-bit with a short context.
  • 32GB leaves about 28GB: 30B-class models at 4-bit with room for a longer context. A 70B model at 4-bit (about 35GB) does not fit.
  • 48GB to 64GB: 70B-class models at 4-bit, tight on 48GB and comfortable on 64GB.

For music. Production Expert's 2026 guide puts MIDI-heavy sessions without large libraries at 16GB to 24GB, mixing with Kontakt libraries at 32GB, and large orchestral templates at 64GB (Production Expert).

For AI video. Most AI video generators run in the cloud, so the Mac's job is editing and export. Size the Mac for your editing app and any local models, not for the generator.

Storage: why 512GB is the real starting point

Benchmarks reported by Digital Trends put the 256GB model's SSD at about half the read speed of the 512GB model, because it uses one storage chip instead of two (Digital Trends). Start at 512GB. Keep sample libraries, raw footage and old projects on an external drive, and back them up.

What owners and reviewers say

BGR calls the M6 fast, quiet and compact, and likes that 2.5Gb Ethernet is now standard. Its complaints: the base model's 16GB and 256GB are thin, upgrades get expensive fast, the power button is still on the bottom, silver is the only color, and Thunderbolt 5 is reserved for the M5 Pro (BGR review). In a MacRumors forum thread, M6 owners report near silence under load; one owner heard a power supply hum on two earlier M4 Pro units. Those are individual reports, not measurements.

Frequently asked questions

Is 16GB enough on a Mac mini for AI music and video?+

For cloud generators and light editing, yes. For local AI models or large sample libraries, 24GB is the safer start, and memory cannot be added later.

Which Mac mini runs local AI models best?+

The M5 Pro with 48GB or 64GB. It has 307GB/s of memory bandwidth against 170GB/s on the M6, and enough memory for 70B-class models at 4-bit.

Should I buy the 256GB Mac mini?+

No, if you can avoid it. Reported benchmarks put its SSD at about half the read speed of the 512GB model, and the storage is soldered, so it cannot be upgraded.

Do I need a 5K monitor for a Mac mini?+

No. A 27-inch 4K monitor works fine. A 5K screen such as the Apple Studio Display makes macOS text sharper, at 218 pixels per inch against about 163.
