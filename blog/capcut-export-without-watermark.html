How to Export From CapCut Without a Watermark

Why does my CapCut export have a watermark?

CapCut's export help page and its pages on template watermarks and watermark-free export name three causes between them, and none is the export button:

CauseWhere it sitsFix
Branded ending clipLast clip on the timeline, added by a template or by defaultSelect it and delete it
Template element with a logoAn overlay, text or sticker layer inside the templateDelete or replace that layer
Premium asset used without ProA Pro-marked sticker, transition, filter or templateSwap it for a free one, or subscribe

If you started from a blank project and used only your own footage, you usually will not see a watermark at all.

How do I remove the ending clip?

The steps are nearly the same everywhere. These are from CapCut's template watermark page.

Phone app (iOS and Android):

  1. Open your project.
  2. Swipe to the end of the timeline.
  3. Tap the CapCut ending clip.
  4. Press Delete.
  5. Check the timeline for any other logo element, then tap Export.

Desktop app:

  1. Open the project and move to the last clip on the timeline.
  2. Select the outro clip and delete it.
  3. Look through every track for overlays or logos left by the template.
  4. Export again.

Web editor:

  1. Open the project in CapCut Online.
  2. Scroll to the end of the timeline.
  3. Click the branded ending clip and delete it.
  4. Preview, then press Export.

What if the watermark is still there?

Then it is inside the edit, not at the end of it. Work through these in order:

  • Check every layer. Templates stack text, stickers and overlays above the video. One of them may carry the mark.
  • Look for Pro icons. A premium sticker, transition or filter can add a watermark on a free account. Replace it with a free one.
  • Replace template stock. Swap any stock clip that came with the template for your own footage.
  • Start clean. CapCut calls editing from scratch the most reliable method. Rebuild the cut in a new project with your own clips, text and audio.

If none of that works, CapCut asks you to contact its support through the help centre.

Do I need CapCut Pro to remove the watermark?

No, not for your own footage. CapCut's help page on watermark-free export lists four routes, and three are free: edit in the normal editor instead of a template, delete the ending clip, or replace premium elements with free ones.

The fourth route is Pro. It makes sense when the premium template or effect is the reason you opened the app. Pro includes those assets, so the watermark tied to them goes away. CapCut's trial page lists watermark-free export, 4K and 60 fps export among Pro benefits, and new subscribers in supported regions get 7 days to try it. If that is your situation, look at CapCut Pro and compare it with what the free plan already covers.

Is a clean free export guaranteed?

No, and CapCut says so. Its guide to the free tier calls the free route a checklist, not a guarantee: you can often leave with a clean video if you stay on free assets and inspect the whole export. Its help centre adds that watermark-free export depends on the features you use and on your app version.

Two things follow. Keep the app updated, since CapCut says the result depends on your app version. And make the check part of every export, not something you do once: a template you used last month may add an ending clip that a blank project never will.

Is it fine to crop or blur the watermark out?

Use the supported routes instead. CapCut's Materials License Agreement says you may not remove copyright or trademark notices and labels that appear on its library materials or on content that includes them. Deleting the ending clip is CapCut's own instruction. Cropping a mark off a premium asset you have not paid for is not.

It also looks worse. A cropped frame loses resolution, and a blurred corner tells every viewer something was hidden there.

What should I check before I post?

Watch the exported file from the first frame to the last, outside the app. A leftover ending clip sits at the very end, which is easy to miss in the editor. Then confirm the picture is sharp at 1080p or whatever you shot in: our guide to CapCut export settings has the resolution and bitrate for each platform.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove the CapCut watermark at the end of my video?

Scroll to the end of the timeline, select the branded ending clip and delete it, then export again. CapCut's help centre gives the same steps for the phone app, the desktop app and the web editor. The ending clip is added by templates, and sometimes by default, and removing it is free.

Can I export from CapCut without a watermark for free?

Yes, in most cases. CapCut's help centre says a project made in the normal editor with your own clips, text and audio exports without a watermark. Trouble starts with templates and premium assets. Delete the ending clip, replace anything Pro-marked with a free item, and the free export is clean.

Why does my CapCut template video still have a watermark?

A logo is probably sitting on a layer inside the template, or the template uses a premium asset. Check every text, sticker and overlay track, replace stock clips with your own footage, and swap Pro-marked effects for free ones. If the template itself is premium, you need Pro to export it without the mark.
