Sora 2 Pro Is Gone: What Happened and What to Use Now (Oct 2026)

By the AI Music Video Guide team · Updated October 25, 2026 · 12 min read

Quick answer: Sora 2 Pro is gone. OpenAI closed the Sora app on April 26, 2026 and removed the sora-2-pro API model on September 24, 2026, with no replacement named. For music videos, Veo 3.1, Kling 4.0 and Seedance 2.5 are the working options; our Sora 2 alternatives guide maps them by job. This page remains as a dated archive note.

What happened to Sora 2 Pro?

OpenAI shut Sora down in two stages: the web and app experiences closed on April 26, 2026, and the API, including the sora-2 and sora-2-pro models and their dated snapshots, was removed on September 24, 2026. OpenAI's deprecation notice lists no recommended replacement for any of the six retired entries, which is unusual for that page.

The announcement came on March 24, 2026, with a notice to API developers the same day. That gave the API the six months OpenAI promises for a generally available model. The consumer side got one month.

The reasons, as The Decoder reported on March 28, 2026, were compute and focus: resources moved toward coding tools and enterprise customers, and Sora continued only as world-model research. OpenAI's launch page for Sora 2 now opens with a banner: "As of April 26, 2026, the Sora product is no longer available." The model lasted just under twelve months as an API and under seven as a consumer app.

Is Sora 2 Pro still available anywhere?

No, not from any source we can verify. OpenAI removed the model from its API on September 24, 2026, Microsoft's Foundry schedule lists its hosted sora-2 build as retired on October 15, 2026, and OpenAI Support told the developer forum on September 8, 2026 that it had no open-weight release plan or timeline to share. Any Pro button still showing is a stale label or a different model.

We have to be plain about one of our own recommendations. Seeddance, the multi-engine bundle we point music-video makers to, still listed Sora 2 and Sora 2 Pro among its engines on its homepage and pricing page on October 21, 2026. We found no public explanation of what sits behind that label now that both upstream routes are closed. Until the platform says which model renders it, treat it as unverified and spend credits on the engines that are current there: Seedance 2, Veo 3, Kling and Wan.

The same caution applies more strongly elsewhere. A search for the model name turns up many sites with Sora in the domain that OpenAI never operated. None of them can be running OpenAI's model today.

The September 8 support reply answered a developer-forum proposal to publish the Sora 2 checkpoints before shutdown, and added that OpenAI hadn't verified any open-weight or self-hosted release.

Can you still get your Sora videos, refunds and credits?

Yes to all three, with limits: exports through OpenAI's export page for as long as it stays open, refunds through the ChatGPT subscription refund process, and purchased ChatGPT or Sora credits spendable in Codex. OpenAI's help article, which showed as updated five days earlier when we read it on October 25, 2026, says any final export window is conditional and that Sora data is permanently deleted once that window passes.

Sign in at the sunset address OpenAI names in the help article, click Export, and wait for the email. The article gives no fixed last day for exports, so don't plan around a grace period.

Kingy AI's migration checklist from September 20, 2026 adds the step most people miss: API outputs weren't in the consumer library, and a saved job ID or temporary URL isn't a backup. Copy finished MP4s into storage you control, keep prompts and references with them, and confirm the files play.

On money, the help article is narrow: it doesn't promise an automatic refund and says nothing about moving credits to another video provider.

Will Sora come back?

Nothing is announced. On October 12, 2026, PYMNTS relayed a report from The Information that OpenAI is in talks with a filmmaker-focused startup co-founded by Jeffrey Katzenberg, former Sora research lead Bill Peebles and former Dropbox CFO Sujay Jaswa, which wants access to Sora for studio software and services. Whether model weights would change hands is unclear, so plan your work as if the answer is no.

The report's stated customers are studios, not individual creators. The app, the feed and the Pro tier aren't coming back in the shape you knew.

What replaces Sora 2 Pro for music videos in October 2026?

Google's Veo 3.1 is the closest match for dialogue and ambience generated with the picture, Kling 4.0, available to everyone since October 15, 2026, is the long-take pick with native 4K and clips up to 30 seconds, Runway Gen-4.5 is the pick for editors, and ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 is the multi-shot storyteller. None of them needs an invite. Our alternatives guide maps each one to a music-video job.

Veo 3.1

Google publishes Veo 3.1 prices per second on the Gemini API pricing page, as of October 21, 2026: $0.40 for Standard with audio at 720p or 1080p, $0.60 at 4K, $0.10 to $0.12 for Fast, and $0.05 to $0.08 for Lite. Lite lets you rough out a sequence at $0.40 per 8-second clip and spend Standard money only on the shots that make the cut.

For subscribers, Google AI Pro was listed at $19.99 a month in Top5Apps' October 6, 2026 roundup, with Veo access through the Gemini app and Flow. Our Veo 3.1 music video tutorial walks it from storyboard to export.

Kling 3.0 and 4.0

Kling 4.0 became available to everyone on October 15, 2026, per Kling's release note, with 3 to 30 second clips, two-channel stereo audio and up to 10 keyframes. Kling's credit guide, published July 28, 2026, lists the Standard plan at $6.99 for the first month and $8.80 on renewal, with 660 credits a month that Kling translates to about 33 videos at 720p. Native 4K costs 30 credits per second, so a 10-second 4K shot is 300 credits, almost half the Standard allowance. Those credit figures were published for the 3.0 series, whose clips run 3 to 15 seconds; check the live generation panel for 4.0 costs.

Two limits. Kling's free Basic tier isn't licensed for commercial use, so a released video needs a paid plan. And native audio carries a credit multiplier the guide doesn't pin to one number, so check the live generation panel before a long shoot. Our Kling vs Seedance comparison has the side-by-side.

Runway Gen-4.5

Runway's pricing page on October 21, 2026 shows Standard at $15 a month or $12 billed annually for 625 credits, Pro at $35 or $28 for 2,250, and Max at $95 or $76 for 9,500 with one month of rollover. Gen-4.5 draws 12 credits per second per Runway's help center, so those plans buy about 52, 187 and 791 seconds a month. The free tier is 125 one-time credits.

Runway's real argument isn't the model. It's that editing tools, the upscaler and third-party models sit in one place; the pricing page lists Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.5 and Nano Banana Pro among the included engines. If your bottleneck is finishing rather than generating, Runway is the sane home.

Seedance 2.5

ByteDance's Seedance line is the one reviewers kept naming when Sora closed, because it kept the two things music-video makers valued most in Sora: stereo audio generated with the picture, and a run of shots with the same character across cuts. Top5Apps' October 6, 2026 ranking puts Seedance, now at 2.5, fourth overall and first for multi-shot narrative. You reach it through Dreamina and CapCut, Runway's plans, or bundles that license it, and each host sets its own credits and commercial terms.

Every paid plan includes a commercial license and watermark-free MP4 export. Seedance 2.5 there costs 12 credits per second at 480p and 23 at 720p, so a 5-second test needs 60 to 115 credits. Where it earns a place: one credit pool across Seedance 2, Veo 3, Kling and Wan, so you can run the same verse through each engine without four subscriptions; and a Music to Video tool that the site says beat-locks cuts to an uploaded MP3 of up to 10 minutes (the page does not name the model behind it), the job every pure text-to-video model still leaves to your editor.

What did Sora 2 Pro do well, and what did it do badly?

Its strength was synchronized sound: dialogue, ambience and effects rendered with the picture in one pass, with physics that mostly held. It did badly on everything around the model: a moving watermark on every app download, short clips, invite gates, regional rollouts, and a Pro tier gated behind the most expensive ChatGPT plan or a per-second API.

We keep the praise on record, in OpenAI's own launch terms. When Sora 2 launched on September 30, 2025, OpenAI described it as more physically accurate, realistic and controllable than prior systems, with synchronized dialogue and sound effects. The cameo system let a consenting person appear across clips with the same face and voice. OpenAI described Sora 2 Pro as its experimental, higher-quality model.

We keep the complaints on record too. The API billed by the second, and its model page has since been deleted, so we won't quote rates we can no longer check.

For a music video there was a deeper problem. Sora's audio was generated, not imported, so it couldn't take your finished track as the clock. Most engines still can't in October 2026, which is why the last step of the plan below is a re-cut.

What should you do if you built a workflow on Sora?

Export first, pick a replacement by job second, and don't pay for any option you can't verify. The export step is the only one with a clock on it; the rest can wait a week while you test one shot per engine on a free tier or the smallest credit pack.

  1. Export your library from the sunset page OpenAI names in its help article, and wait for the ready email. Download API outputs separately into storage you control.
  2. Save the ingredients, not just the results: prompts, reference images, aspect ratios, camera notes and the edit project that ties the clips to your song.
  3. Match the engine to the shot. Dialogue and ambience: Veo 3.1. High-motion, 4K and takes up to 30 seconds: Kling 4.0. Multi-shot sequences with one character: Seedance 2.5. Finishing and editing: Runway.
  4. Test one representative shot on a free tier or the smallest credit pack before buying a plan. Runway's 125 one-time credits cover two 5-second Gen-4.5 clips and Kling has a free tier; Seeddance's one daily credit is not enough for a single render, and Veo's API has no free tier.
  5. Treat any option still labelled with the retired model as a question to ask the vendor, not a feature to pay for.
  6. Re-cut to the song. Whatever engine you choose, the track is the master clock. Use a beat-lock option if the tool has one, or cut on the grid in your editor.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sora 2 Pro still available anywhere in October 2026?+

No. OpenAI removed sora-2 and sora-2-pro from its API on September 24, 2026, Microsoft's Foundry schedule lists its hosted sora-2 build as retired on October 15, 2026, and OpenAI Support said in September 2026 it had no open-weight release plan to share. Any site still offering a Sora 2 Pro option is showing a stale label or a different model, so ask the vendor what actually renders before paying.

Can I still download my old Sora videos?+

Yes, for now. OpenAI's help article says you can export your library from the sunset page it names, then wait for an email when the archive is ready. There's no fixed last day for exports, any final export window is conditional, and data is permanently deleted once that window passes. Export today and keep a second copy somewhere else.

Will OpenAI bring Sora back?+

Nothing is announced. On October 12, 2026, PYMNTS relayed a report from The Information that OpenAI is in talks with a film-focused startup co-founded by Jeffrey Katzenberg, Bill Peebles and Sujay Jaswa about licensing Sora technology for studio tools. Whether weights would transfer is unclear. Even if it happens, it would be a studio product, not the consumer app or API you used.

What is the closest replacement for Sora 2 Pro's synced audio?+

Google's Veo 3.1 generates dialogue, ambience and effects with the picture in one pass and is priced on the Gemini API at $0.40 per second for Standard at 720p or 1080p as of October 21, 2026. Kling 4.0 and Seedance 2.5 also produce native audio. For a music video your finished track still has to be the clock, so plan to cut to the song or use a beat-lock tool.

Do I get a refund for my Sora or ChatGPT credits?+

OpenAI's help article routes refund questions through the standard ChatGPT subscription refund process and doesn't promise an automatic refund. Purchased ChatGPT or Sora credits can still be spent in Codex. There's no stated path for moving credits to another video provider, so if you paid for a top ChatGPT tier mainly for video, raise it as a subscription refund request.

Why does a platform still show a Sora 2 Pro option?+

Most likely because the label was never removed after OpenAI's API closed on September 24, 2026 and Microsoft's hosted copy reached its October 15, 2026 retirement date. The reseller's homepage still listed the model when we checked on October 21, 2026. Treat such an option as unverified, ask the platform which model renders it, and spend credits on engines that are current, such as Seedance 2, Veo and Kling.
