Holiday Gift Guide for Beauty and Skincare: 10 Useful Ideas

By the AI Music Video GuideUpdated November 11, 20264 min read

Quick answer: This guide is for anyone shopping for a friend or relative who likes skincare, hair and beauty. The three best picks are a sunscreen they will wear, an appointment voucher, and a free evening at home.

Most ideas cost little, some cost nothing, and all come from any shop. Nothing on this page earns the studio a commission. Prices appear only where we read them on a page we link.

Ten gift ideas for beauty

1. An evening at home

Free

Offer a night of manicures, hair braiding or a pamper hour at your place, with the phones away.

Suits: Friends and family who rarely take time for themselves. Check before buying: Agree a date and bring your own supplies.

2. A sunscreen they will wear

Low to mid price

The American Academy of Dermatology recommends SPF 30 or higher, broad spectrum (UVA and UVB), and says water resistant means 40 or 80 minutes of staying on wet or sweaty skin. Not all sunscreens are water resistant. Source: American Academy of Dermatology.

Suits: Anyone who is outdoors, or sits near a window. Check before buying: Fragrance and skin sensitivity (ask first), the finish, and the label words above. See our face sunscreen guide.

3. A satin or silk pillowcase

Low to mid price

A soft pillowcase is a comfortable gift with no routine attached.

Suits: Anyone who sleeps with long or textured hair. Check before buying: The material on the label, the size of their pillow and care instructions.

4. A wide-tooth comb or detangling brush

A few dollars

A good comb is a small gift people keep for years.

Suits: Anyone with long, curly or textured hair. Check before buying: Tooth spacing and handle shape for their hair type.

5. Claw clips and soft hair ties

A few dollars

Easy, cheap and used daily. Pick shapes that hold the hair they have.

Suits: Anyone with medium to long hair. Check before buying: Size of the clip against hair thickness.

6. Nail clippers and a glass file

A few dollars

A sharp pair of clippers and a fine file make home nail care tidier.

Suits: Anyone. Check before buying: Handle comfort and a case that protects the blades.

7. A makeup brush set

Low to mid price

A few good brushes beat a large set. Choose the shapes they use.

Suits: People who wear makeup most days. Check before buying: Which shapes they use, synthetic or natural bristles, and a returns policy.

8. A salon, nail or brow appointment voucher

Varies

A booked appointment is a treat they do not have to organise or pay for.

Suits: Anyone who has said they would like to go. Check before buying: The salon's voucher terms, expiry date and cancellation policy.

9. A hair dryer with lower heat settings

Mid to high price

Heat from dryers can dry hair. A dryer with adjustable heat gives choice.

Suits: Anyone who dries their hair daily. Check before buying: Heat and speed settings, weight, and warranty. See our hair dryer guide.

10. A fragrance sample set

Low to mid price

Small vials let them find a scent before committing to a bottle.

Suits: People who like to try new scents. Check before buying: Allergies and strong preferences. Ask first, and pick a retailer with a gift receipt.

What not to buy

Skip strong skincare actives such as retinol unless they asked for them: our retinol guide explains why beginners go slowly. Skip strongly scented products when you do not know their tastes or allergies. Skip anything that promises to erase wrinkles or scars, and skip unbranded imitations of a product they love. Always include a gift receipt.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good beauty gift if I do not know their skin?

Pick something that does not touch skin chemistry: a comb, clips, a satin pillowcase, a voucher or a free evening at home. Ask before buying products with fragrance or active ingredients.

What sunscreen should I look for?

The American Academy of Dermatology recommends SPF 30 or higher, broad spectrum, and water resistant where it applies. Check the label.

What can I give for under $10?

Claw clips, hair ties, nail clippers and a glass file, or a wide-tooth comb. A free option is an evening of manicures at home.

Should I buy skincare actives as a gift?

Only if they asked. Strong ingredients can irritate, and people choose them by their own skin history.
