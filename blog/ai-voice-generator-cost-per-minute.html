What is a credit, and how many minutes of speech does one buy?

A credit is the unit the vendor bills in; the help center says credits 'were previously referred to as "characters."' Text to speech on V2 Multilingual models costs 1 credit per character, and the pricing page prints each plan's allowance as an approximate number of minutes: 10k credits is about 10 minutes (ElevenLabs pricing page and help center, read November 13, 2026).

The rate depends on the model and the route. The pricing page lists 1 credit per character on V2 Multilingual models, and 0.5 to 1 credit per character for V2 Flash/Turbo English and V2.5 Flash/Turbo Multilingual API usage (ElevenLabs pricing page, read November 13, 2026). The help center adds that credit use depends on your plan, whether you generate on the website or through the API, the product and the model (ElevenLabs help center, read November 13, 2026).

Our arithmetic from those two printed facts: at 1 credit per character, with 10k credits equal to about 10 minutes, one spoken minute is about 1,000 credits. That is the base rate behind every text to speech row in our table below. The vendor prints the minutes as approximate, so treat 1,000 credits per minute as a planning number, not a meter reading.

Each API request also has a character ceiling. The text to speech page lists, per API request, 5,000 characters for Eleven v3, 10,000 for Multilingual v2 and 40,000 for Flash v2.5; for Eleven v4 it says 5,000 in the model section but 10,000 in the FAQ, so the page disagrees with itself (ElevenLabs text to speech page, read November 13, 2026). The help center's credits page explains the unit from the account side.

How many credits does each feature cost per minute?

Speech costs about 1,000 credits a minute by our arithmetic; the rest are printed rates. Speech to Text is about 330 credits per minute, Eleven Music about 900, Voice Changer and Voice Isolator about 1,000 each, and dubbing runs from about 2,000 to 10,000 credits per minute depending on mode and watermark (ElevenLabs pricing page, read November 13, 2026).

The per-feature rates on the pricing page, read November 13, 2026:

  • Text to Speech: 1 credit per character on V2 Multilingual models; 0.5 to 1 credit per character for V2 Flash/Turbo English and V2.5 Flash/Turbo Multilingual API usage.
  • Speech to Text: about 330 credits per minute.
  • Eleven Music: about 900 credits per minute.
  • Sound Effects: about 200 credits per generation.
  • Voice Changer: about 1,000 credits per minute.
  • Voice Isolator: about 1,000 credits per minute.
  • Dubbing: about 2,000 credits per minute automatic with watermark, 3,000 automatic without, 5,000 in Dubbing Studio with watermark, 10,000 in Dubbing Studio without.

Source for the list: ElevenLabs pricing page, read November 13, 2026. The full grid, with the per-feature rates under the plan cards, is on the ElevenLabs pricing page.

The Voice Isolator page says the same rate in the older unit: 'Voice Isolator costs 1000 characters for every minute of audio.' It takes files up to 500MB and 1 hour long, in WAV, MP3, FLAC, OGG and AAC, and its FAQ says it is not designed for music vocals, though you may have success depending on the content (ElevenLabs voice isolator page, read November 13, 2026).

Dubbing: use the dubbing page's included minutes

Dubbing is the one feature where we refuse to convert credits into minutes ourselves, because the dubbing studio page already prints included minutes per plan and they do not line up neatly with the credit rates. For Dubbing v2 it lists 0.4 minutes included on Free, 2 on Starter, 9 on Creator and 44 on Pro, with extra minutes at $4.91, $2.70, $2.45 and $2.23 in the same order (ElevenLabs dubbing studio page, read November 13, 2026).

The same page shows Automatic Dubbing v1 rows with watermark at 5 minutes for $0.73 an extra minute, 15 at $0.40, 61 at $0.36 and 300 at $0.33, without naming the plans on those rows, and three without-watermark values for four plans, so the mapping is unclear and we leave it there (ElevenLabs dubbing studio page, read November 13, 2026). The dubbing studio page says Dubbing v2 works from the original audio and handles translation, cloning, dubbing and sync automatically.

What does one video's voice track cost in credits?

Our arithmetic, using the pricing page's rates: a 30-second intro is about 500 credits, a 2-minute narration about 2,000, and a 10-minute explainer about 10,000, which is the whole Free month. A 3-minute music bed is about 2,700 credits and cleaning 20 minutes of interview audio with Voice Isolator about 20,000 (ElevenLabs pricing page rates, read November 13, 2026).

Which plan gives the cheapest minute?

Of the three plans we computed, Pro is cheapest per credit at monthly list price; we did not compute Scale or Business, and annual billing lowers every figure. Our arithmetic: Starter's $6 for 30k credits is $0.20 per 1,000 credits, Creator's $22 for 121k about $0.18, Pro's $99 for 600k $0.165. The vendor's extra-minute prices run from about $0.36 on Free to $0.17 on Pro (ElevenLabs pricing page, read November 13, 2026).

Do unused credits roll over, and when do they expire?

The pricing page says your credit allowance resets at the start of each billing cycle, which begins on the day you subscribed, and that, with an active paid subscription, unused credits roll over for up to two months, up to 2x your monthly quota. Downgrade or cancel and unused credits are not rolled over (ElevenLabs pricing page and help center, read November 13, 2026).

The help center says the same from the account side: monthly plans 'receive a set amount of credits each month, which reset monthly', 'Up to two months' worth of unused credits can roll over to the next month', and on downgrade or cancellation 'this does not take effect until the end of your current billing cycle, and unused credits are not rolled over' (ElevenLabs help center, read November 13, 2026).

The pricing page's own line is 'Unused credits roll over for up to two months, up to 2x your monthly quota', and it adds that the balance can reach at most 3x the monthly quota while a paid subscription is active (ElevenLabs pricing page, read November 13, 2026).

Is pay-per-use API pricing cheaper than a plan?

Per thousand characters, the API list price sits below our per-credit plan arithmetic, assuming 1 credit per character, which the pricing page prints for V2 Multilingual models; the pages we read do not print a v4 plan credit rate. Text to speech costs $0.08 per 1K characters on v4 and $0.04 on v4 Turbo; Voice Isolator, Voice Changer and Sound Effects are $0.12 a minute, Music $0.15, and Dubbing v2 $2.20 per minute (ElevenLabs API pricing page, read November 13, 2026).

The API pricing page is a separate price list from the plans, with the older models and the dubbing modes priced line by line.

Our arithmetic on that side: 10 minutes of text to speech is about 10,000 characters at the planning rate above, which at $0.08 per 1K characters on v4 is $0.80, and 20 minutes of Voice Isolator at $0.12 a minute is $2.40 (ElevenLabs API pricing page rates, read November 13, 2026). Set that against Starter's credits at $0.20 per 1,000 at list, also our arithmetic. A plan also carries voice slots, concurrent requests and Studio projects; the API list is usage only.

There is a third route. The help center says Pay As You Go lets you prepay for credits without a monthly subscription, with the balance stored as a dollar value and converted at your current plan's rate (ElevenLabs help center, read November 13, 2026). Neither the help center nor the pricing page we read prints that rate, so we cannot tell you what a prepaid minute costs, and we will not guess.

What else the pages do not say: how many credits a Studio project consumes beyond the per-feature rates, and whether the 'about N minutes' figure assumes a particular model. When you need a firm number for a client quote, run a short test on the Free plan and read the credit counter, then scale from there with the plan you pick through our link.

Frequently asked questions

How many credits does one minute of AI speech use?

Roughly 1,000 credits per spoken minute, our arithmetic from two printed facts: text to speech costs 1 credit per character on the standard multilingual models, and the vendor prints 10k credits as about 10 minutes. The vendor calls the minutes approximate, and Flash and Turbo API usage is listed at 0.5 to 1 credit per character, so a fast model can cost less.

Do unused credits expire at the end of the month?

With an active paid subscription, not immediately. The pricing page says unused credits roll over for up to two months, up to 2x your monthly quota, and that the balance can reach at most 3x the quota while a paid subscription is active. The allowance resets at the start of each billing cycle. If you downgrade or cancel, the help center says unused credits are not rolled over.

Is annual billing worth it for a voice plan?

The pricing page describes annual billing as paying for 10 months and getting 2 free, which is why the annual monthly figures are lower: $5 instead of $6 on Starter, $18.33 instead of $22 on Creator, $82.50 instead of $99 on Pro. It pays off if you expect to stay subscribed for the full year, since the saving is the two free months.

What does a 2-minute narration cost on the cheapest paid plan?

About 2,000 credits by our arithmetic (1,000 credits per spoken minute from the vendor's 1 credit per character and 10k credits as about 10 minutes). That is 6.7 percent of the $6 Starter plan's 30k monthly credits, or 1.7 percent of the $22 Creator plan's 121k. At list price Starter's credits work out to $0.20 per 1,000, also our arithmetic.

Can I buy voice minutes without a subscription?

Two ways. The help center describes Pay As You Go, where you prepay for credits without a monthly plan and the balance is stored as a dollar value converted at your current plan's rate; the pages do not print that rate. The API page lists pay-per-use prices instead, such as $0.08 per 1K characters of text to speech on v4 and $0.12 a minute for Voice Isolator.

How many minutes of dubbing does each plan include?

Use the dubbing page's own numbers rather than a credit conversion. For Dubbing v2 it lists 0.4 minutes included on Free, 2 on Starter, 9 on Creator and 44 on Pro, with extra minutes at $4.91, $2.70, $2.45 and $2.23 in that order. The pricing page's credit rates for dubbing run from 2,000 to 10,000 credits per minute depending on mode and watermark.
