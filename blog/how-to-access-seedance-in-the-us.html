Quick answer: you don't need a VPN, a Chinese phone number, or a workaround app. ByteDance region-locks Seedance 2.0 inside its own Chinese apps, but it licenses the model to web platforms that accept normal email or Google sign-ins — and those work from the US today. Pick a licensed platform, sign up with your email, and you're generating within five minutes.

Why is Seedance 2.0 region-locked in the first place?

Seedance 2.0 is ByteDance's video model, released February 12, 2026. ByteDance's own consumer apps that host it — Jimeng and Doubao — require a mainland Chinese (+86) phone number to register, which is the wall US, Canadian, and European creators hit. As WeShop AI's access guide puts it, the +86 verification is the roadblock — not your location. The model itself is licensed internationally.

The rule of thumb (from GlbGPT's availability breakdown): if a platform demands a +86 phone number, it inherits ByteDance's region lock. If it accepts email or Google sign-in, it bypasses the lock entirely.

What are the three ways to use Seedance 2.0 from the US?

US creators have three routes: ByteDance's own apps (Jimeng and Doubao), which require a +86 Chinese phone number and are impractical from the US; licensed web platforms, which accept plain email or Google sign-in and include the full creator UI; and API providers such as fal.ai, Replicate, and Segmind, which bill per generation in USD. Frequently asked questions

Do I need a VPN to use Seedance 2.0 in the US?+

No. ByteDance licenses Seedance 2.0 to web platforms that serve the US directly. A VPN only matters if you try to register on ByteDance's mainland-China apps (Jimeng, Doubao), which US creators don't need to do — and a VPN can actually trigger payment fraud checks on legitimate platforms.

Does Seedance 2.0 require a Chinese phone number?+

Only ByteDance's own Chinese apps require +86 phone verification. Licensed international platforms and API providers (fal.ai, Replicate, Segmind) accept plain email or Google sign-in with no phone number at all. If a site demands a +86 number, use a different platform.

Is Seedance 2.0 free to use in the US?+

There's a real free tier: one credit that refreshes every 24 hours, which is not enough for a single video render (the shortest Seedance 2.5 clip costs 48 credits). Free renders are watermarked and not licensed for commercial use — watermark-free output and a commercial license come with paid plans, which start around $9.90/month as of July 2026.

Is it legal to use Seedance from the US?+

Yes. The platforms offering Seedance 2.0 internationally license the model for distribution — you're a normal customer of a licensed service, the same as using any AI video tool. Check each platform's terms for commercial-use rights, which typically require a paid plan.
