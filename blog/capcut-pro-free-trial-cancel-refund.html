CapCut Pro Free Trial: How It Works, and How to Cancel Before You Are Charged

Quick answer: CapCut offers a 7-day free trial of Pro to new subscribers in supported regions. Start it from the Upgrade or Join Pro button; Pro features switch on at once. The plan renews by itself, so cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends if you do not want to pay.

Who can get the CapCut Pro free trial?

New subscribers in countries where CapCut offers it. Its trial conditions page lists four checks:

  • The trial is available in selected countries and regions. If you do not see the option, it is not offered where you are.
  • The app must be version 8.20 or higher.
  • The account must not have subscribed to Pro before. CapCut's other trial page counts an earlier trial too.
  • A Free Trial badge appears on the subscription page when the offer applies to you.

On the phone, the region is set by your App Store or Play Store country, not by where you are standing.

How do I start the trial?

These steps are from CapCut's free trial help page.

Web:

  1. Sign in to CapCut Online.
  2. Click your profile icon, then Upgrade to Pro.
  3. Look for a Start 7-Day Free Trial button in place of Subscribe.
  4. Click it and add a payment method when asked.
  5. Pro switches on immediately and the 7 days start counting.

Phone app:

  1. Update the app, open it and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap Join CapCut Pro next to your nickname.
  3. Tap the trial button and confirm with the App Store or Google Play.

Desktop app: click to upgrade inside the app. It opens the web subscription page in your browser, where you complete the same steps. The desktop app then picks up your Pro status.

CapCut says the trial gives full Pro access, including premium templates, export with no watermark, and 4K and 60 fps export. What each plan includes is in our plans guide.

Do I need a card to start it?

Expect to be asked for one. CapCut's two pages differ here. The pricing page says no credit card is required to begin the trial. The help page says the web flow asks for a valid payment method to verify eligibility, and that on a phone your store account is charged automatically after 7 days unless you cancel.

Both pages add that availability varies by region, platform and billing channel. Go by what your own checkout shows, and assume a charge will follow the trial unless you act.

How do I avoid being charged?

Cancel in time, in the right place. CapCut's page on forgotten cancellations says subscriptions renew automatically and that you need to cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Miss that window and a new billing period starts.

CapCut's plan guide confirms that a subscription is cancelled through the platform it was bought on. Two habits help: set a reminder for day 5 of the trial, and take a screenshot of the cancellation confirmation.

If the cancel option is missing or fails, CapCut's auto-renewal help page asks you to contact support with your login method, device type, a screenshot of the problem and your order number or receipt.

Can I get a refund from CapCut?

Usually not. CapCut's help page says subscriptions are virtual goods and purchases generally do not support refunds, because the features are available the moment you pay. It leaves one door open: for special circumstances such as an accidental double charge, you can submit an appeal with your account details and receipts.

CapCut's pages do not promise an outcome, so treat a refund as unlikely and cancel in time instead.

What if I paid and Pro is still locked?

It is almost always the wrong account. CapCut's troubleshooting page explains that each login method (TikTok, email, Google, Apple ID) creates a separate account, even with the same email address. Sign out and sign back in with the exact method you used when you paid.

Other causes CapCut lists: a sync delay of a few minutes on desktop, or a region where Pro is restricted. Its second troubleshooting page adds an outdated app or browser, and calls the web membership page the most accurate view of your status, so check there first.

Is the trial worth starting?

Start it when you have a real project ready, not before. Seven days go quickly, and the useful test is whether Pro saves you time on work you were going to do anyway: a release clip, a week of shop videos, a 4K export.

If the free plan already covers that work, you have your answer without a trial. Our guide to what the free plan includes lays out the difference. When you are ready, check whether the trial is offered on your account.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the CapCut Pro free trial?

Seven days. CapCut offers it to new subscribers in supported countries and regions, and Pro features switch on as soon as you start. When the 7 days end, the subscription renews into a paid plan automatically unless you have cancelled at least 24 hours beforehand.

Why don't I see the CapCut Pro free trial?

CapCut lists four conditions. The trial must be offered in your country or region, the app must be version 8.20 or higher, and the account must never have subscribed to Pro, including an earlier trial. A Free Trial badge appears on the subscription page when the trial is available to you.

How do I cancel CapCut Pro before the trial ends?

Cancel on the platform where you subscribed, at least 24 hours before renewal. On an iPhone, open Settings, your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open the Play Store, then Payments and subscriptions. For a web purchase, use the membership page in CapCut Online. Keep a screenshot of the confirmation.

Does CapCut give refunds?

Generally no. CapCut's help centre says subscriptions are virtual goods and purchases do not normally support refunds. For special circumstances, such as an accidental double charge, you can submit an appeal with your account information and receipts. Cancelling at least 24 hours before renewal is the reliable way to avoid the charge.

I paid for CapCut Pro but it is still locked. What do I do?

Sign out and sign back in with the exact login method you used to pay. CapCut says each method, such as TikTok, email, Google or Apple ID, creates a separate account. Also allow a few minutes for desktop to sync, update the app, and check your status on the web membership page.
