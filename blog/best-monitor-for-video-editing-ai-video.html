Short answer: for AI video and music videos that people watch on phones, a calibrated 27-inch 4K sRGB monitor covers the job: the Dell S2725QC or, for wide color, the ASUS ProArt PA279CRV. On a Mac, the Apple Studio Display gives the sharpest text. Spend on HDR (Studio Display XDR or an OLED like the ASUS PA32UCDM) only if you deliver HDR.

Four rules before you buy

  • Match the screen to where people watch. Social and YouTube video is mostly watched in sRGB. Accurate sRGB matters more than a wide gamut you will not deliver in.
  • Ignore entry-level HDR badges. DisplayHDR 400 does not make an HDR grading monitor; PCWorld found the PA279CRV's HDR weak and advises against it for HDR work (PCWorld).
  • On a Mac, 5K at 27 inches is the sharp option. Apple's 5K panel is 218 pixels per inch (Apple); a 27-inch 4K panel is about 163, and macOS scales it. AppleInsider found 4K text less crisp on a Mac (AppleInsider).
  • Check the charging watts for one-cable laptops. The PA279CRV gives 96W and the U2725QE 140W. PCWorld notes 96W may not fully power a Windows laptop with a dedicated GPU (PCWorld).

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a 4K monitor for editing AI video?+

It helps. A 27-inch 4K screen shows a 1080p timeline with room for panels and lets you check 4K exports at full detail. Most AI video is watched on phones, so accurate sRGB matters more than resolution beyond that.

Is a DisplayHDR 400 monitor good for HDR editing?+

No. Entry-level HDR badges do not deliver the brightness or local dimming HDR grading needs. Use a mini-LED or OLED monitor if you deliver HDR.

What is the best monitor for video editing on a Mac?+

For sharp text, a 5K display such as the Apple Studio Display. For HDR, the Studio Display XDR. A 27-inch 4K monitor works too, but macOS scales it and text looks softer.

Is OLED safe for video editing?+

It gives the best blacks, but static toolbars risk burn-in over time. Check the warranty; ASUS covers burn-in for 3 years on the PA32UCDM.
