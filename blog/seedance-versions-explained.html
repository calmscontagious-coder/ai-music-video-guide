What is Seedance and who makes it?

Seedance is an AI text-to-video model family built by ByteDance, the company behind TikTok, and first released in June 2025, according to Wikipedia (read October 25, 2026). MindStudio places it inside ByteDance's wider "Seed" model family next to Seed-TTS and Seed-ASR. ByteDance's Seed research team publishes the models and their launch notes.

Wikipedia's summary is that Seedance 2.0 "quickly went viral" for photorealistic clips of famous actors and characters, and that the realism raised concerns about copyright, disinformation and the careers of human performers.

BBC News (dated February 19, 2026 in Wikipedia's citation; read October 25, 2026) quotes AI ethics researcher Margaret Mitchell saying the model stands out because it combines text, visuals and audio in a single system. It also quotes the line that stuck: "For the first time, I'm not thinking that this looks good for AI. Instead, I'm thinking that this looks straight out of a real production pipeline," said Jan-Willem Blom of the creative studio Videostate.

What changed in Seedance 2.0?

Seedance 2.0 made the name famous. BBC News reported it generates cinema-quality video "complete with sound effects and dialogue" from a few written prompts, and Atlas Cloud's April 20, 2026 release lists clips up to 15 seconds, output up to 1080p and up to 12 reference files in one generation. It also drew cease-and-desist letters from Disney and Paramount.

The viral clips came first. Wikipedia lists Friends characters reimagined as otters, a Brad Pitt versus Tom Cruise fight, and Will Smith battling a spaghetti monster. Then the legal response: Disney sent a cease-and-desist letter on February 13, 2026, Paramount Skydance alleged "blatant infringement", and on February 16, 2026 ByteDance said it "respects intellectual property rights" and would strengthen safeguards. On March 16, 2026 two US senators asked ByteDance to shut the model down.

The technical shift was the reference system. Atlas Cloud describes a "Universal Reference" setup that takes up to 12 input files at once, each bound to one element of the output, such as a face or a camera move. Atlas also claims a usable-output rate near 90 percent against an industry average it puts near 20 percent; that is a reseller's own number with no independent check.

What changed in Seedance 2.5?

Seedance 2.5, launched July 31, 2026, doubled the single-pass length from 15 to 30 seconds, added multi-round extension, and raised the reference ceiling to 30 images, 10 video clips and 10 audio clips in one pass, per ByteDance's Seed team. It also added timestamp-level editing, so one stretch of a clip can be changed without regenerating all of it.

ByteDance frames 2.5 as a shift "from merely generating a clip to completing a creative work". It builds on the audio-video architecture of 2.0 and adds clay-render referencing, where a textureless 3D mock-up sets blocking and camera paths, plus green-screen and camera-perspective editing. The launch post's own example is a one-take shot of a singer walking from a dressing room through a backstage corridor onto an arena stage, the kind of brief a music video needs.

Wikipedia adds a beta long-video mode "extending clips to 3 minutes", citing a July 4, 2026 pre-launch article, and third-party API providers such as Wiro AI. ByteDance's own post does not name a 3-minute mode; it says users can produce "multi-minute content" by extending a 30-second clip in rounds. Treat the 3-minute figure as a pre-launch report, not a published spec.

ByteDance also names the weak spots: "still room for improvement, particularly regarding the physical plausibility of complex motions and the stability of scenes involving interactions among multiple subjects." Where do the sources disagree on dates?

They agree on June 2025 and July 31, 2026 but blur the middle. Wikipedia gives only "February 2026" for 2.0 and cites Reuters coverage dated February 13; the Seedance2ai.online release says February 12. MindStudio's explainer, which Wikipedia dates to June 24, 2026, describes 2.5 five weeks before ByteDance's launch and says it was rolling out "through mid-to-late 2025".

The MindStudio page carries no visible date (read October 25, 2026), and compares 2.5 with 1.0 rather than with 2.0. Its 30-second and 50-reference figures match ByteDance's post, so the specs hold up; its timeline does not. BBC's "eight months later" from June 2025 lands in February 2026 and agrees with Wikipedia. Where a dated primary post and a secondary page disagree, our table uses the primary post's date.

Why are so many "Seedance" websites not ByteDance?

Because ByteDance shipped the model inside its own apps, Jimeng AI and Doubao Pro, and Atlas Cloud notes that Jimeng requires Chinese credentials, so third parties filled the gap. The February 18, 2026 "New Seedance 2.0 Platform" press release came from a site called Seedance2ai.online, not ByteDance, and Atlas Cloud, MindStudio and seeddance.video are all resellers or integrators.

That press release is a useful specimen. It is a paid release, it says the model "currently lives inside Chinese-language apps - Dreamina and Doubao", it describes a platform "built on ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 model", and it quotes its own plan prices and video counts, none of which describe ByteDance's product. Read any "Seedance" site the same way: find the sentence that says whose model it runs, and treat its prices as that site's prices. Frequently asked questions

Is Seedance 2.5 the newest Seedance version?+

Yes. ByteDance's Seed team dated its official launch post July 31, 2026, and Wikipedia lists 2.5 as the stable release as of October 25, 2026. It generates 30 seconds in one pass and extends in rounds. No source in our set names a later numbered version, so any site advertising a newer number is making its own claim.

How is Seedance 2.0 different from Seedance 2.5?+

Two numbers carry most of it. Seedance 2.0 generated up to 15 seconds and accepted up to 12 reference files, per Atlas Cloud's April 2026 release. Seedance 2.5 generates up to 30 seconds and accepts 30 images, 10 video clips and 10 audio clips, per ByteDance. Version 2.5 also added timestamp-level editing and clay-render referencing.

When exactly did Seedance 2.0 come out?+

February 2026 is certain; the day is less so. Wikipedia says February 2026 and cites Reuters coverage dated February 13, 2026. Can Seedance 2.5 make a three-minute video?+
