How do I cut footage to my song?

With Auto Cut. CapCut's help page describes it as a tool that trims and arranges footage to match an audio track, with three modes: Beat Sync for music, Speech Pause Detection for talking, and a script-based mode. It is on the phone app and the desktop app, not the web editor, and CapCut says it is still rolling out by region.

On desktop, per CapCut's how-to page:

  1. Open a project with your clips on the timeline and your song as the audio.
  2. Open the AI tools and choose Auto Cut.
  3. Pick Beat Sync.
  4. Adjust the sensitivity if offered, for example every beat or strong beats only.
  5. Apply, then review the result on the timeline.

In the phone app, CapCut places Auto Cut under the Templates icon, in the AI section. Choose the music option and pick the song file from your device.

Treat the output as a rough cut. Drag clips into a better order, delete cuts that land badly, and save your strongest image for the chorus. For cutting by hand, with markers on each beat, our two guides go step by step: beat-sync editing and beat-synced cuts for AI clips.

How do I put lyrics on screen?

Generate captions, then fix them against your lyric sheet. CapCut says auto captions rely on speech recognition, so treat the result on a sung vocal as a draft. The captions come out synced to playback, which is the part that saves work.

Steps and fixes are in our auto captions guide. For a dedicated lyric video, see how to make a lyric video.

Can I use my own song, and what about CapCut's music?

Your own song is the right audio. CapCut's Materials License Agreement says that when you add music that did not come from its licensed libraries, you confirm that you created it or hold the rights and permissions. For an artist with their own release, that is a box you can tick honestly.

CapCut's own libraries are a different matter:

  • Sounds, the general library, are for personal, non-commercial videos. Promoting a release is promotion, so do not lean on them.
  • Commercial Sounds may be used by businesses and advertisers, but only in videos shown within 3 places: CapCut, TikTok and TikTok for Business.

The same care applies to stickers, fonts and templates if a clip is promotional: use items marked for commercial use. The details are in CapCut commercial use rules, and what the platforms ask of AI-made video is in our platform rules guide.

What should I export for each platform?

Two masters cover most releases. For vertical video, CapCut's Reels guide recommends 1080 x 1920 at 30 fps, with 4,000 to 6,000 Kbps at 1080p. For YouTube, YouTube Help recommends 8 Mbps for 1080p at 24 to 30 fps and 12 Mbps at 48 to 60 fps for standard dynamic range uploads, in the frame rate you recorded.

Before posting, delete any branded ending clip a template added. The checks are in our export settings guide and our watermark guide.

What clips does one release need?

A workable set from one song:

  • A full-length video, horizontal, for YouTube.
  • Two or three vertical teasers cut from the strongest sections, each starting on the hook.
  • A lyric clip of the chorus.
  • A short looping visual for streaming profiles; see our guide to making a Spotify Canvas.

Our guide one song, twenty videos stretches the same footage further, and the TikTok promotion guide covers what to do with the teasers once they exist. If you edit on a phone only, start with the phone workflow.

Frequently asked questions

Is CapCut good for making music videos?

It suits the editing stage. CapCut can cut footage to a track with Auto Cut, add captions for lyrics, and export vertical and horizontal versions. It does not write the song or shoot the footage. The free plan covers basic cutting, text and export of your own clips.

How do I sync video to the beat in CapCut?

Use Auto Cut and choose the music option, called Beat Sync on desktop. CapCut analyses the audio and trims and arranges your clips to match it, and you then adjust the result on the timeline. Auto Cut is available in the phone app and the desktop app, not in the web editor, and is rolling out by region.

Can I use my own song in a CapCut video?

Yes. CapCut's licence says that when you add music that did not come from its licensed libraries, you confirm you created it or hold the rights. CapCut's general Sounds library is for personal, non-commercial videos, so a release clip should use your own track.

Can I use CapCut's music library to promote my release?

Not the general Sounds library, which is licensed for personal, non-commercial videos. Commercial Sounds may be used for business purposes, but only in videos shown within 3 places: CapCut, TikTok and TikTok for Business. For a release, your own song is the audio you should be using anyway.

What export settings should musicians use in CapCut?
